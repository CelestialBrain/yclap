## TRUNK — the problem

**Recyclable and organic waste from Ateneo and its surrounding Katipunan–Marikina-fringe
community is thrown as residual instead of diverted — even though it has market value
and a nearby buyer.**

One sentence for the slide:

> *Waste that could be sold and diverted is being landfilled, because nobody at the
> point of disposal can see its price or its path to a collector.*

Why this passes the "urgent + campus or surrounding community" test:
- It is **in the room** — every canteen, dorm, and org event produces it daily.
- It is **climate**, not just housekeeping: landfilled organics generate **methane**, a
  short-lived, high-GWP gas, and the waste sector is a named PAM in the Philippine NDC.
- It is **ours to act on** in five weeks. We cannot rebuild flood control by Sep 12; we
  can move kilograms.

---

## ROOTS — causes (why is this problem in place?)

| # | Root cause | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **Price opacity at the point of disposal** | Scrap has a real ₱/kg value, but the student or household holding the bottle cannot see it. With no visible value, the material is treated as garbage by default. |
| **R2** | **Broken last link to collectors** | Junkshops, MRFs, and informal collectors already recover material — but finding one, knowing what they accept, and reaching them is high-friction. The recovery capacity exists; the routing does not. |
| **R3** | **Segregation is a rule, not a habit** | RA 9003 mandates ecological solid waste management, but on-ground enforcement and follow-through are weak, and bins that are segregated at the top are often recombined downstream — so sorting feels pointless. |
| **R4** | **Waste is framed as sanitation, not as climate** | Disposal is somebody else's job (janitorial, LGU). Because nobody counts the carbon or the kilograms, there is no feedback loop and no accountability — the diversion rate is invisible, so it is unmanaged. |

**Deepest root (say this if asked "why?" a fourth time):** the material's value and its
climate cost are both **unpriced and unmeasured** at the moment of the decision.

---

## BRANCHES — consequences (what happens if this is not addressed?)

| Category | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Environmental** | Organics rot anaerobically in landfill → **methane**; recyclables that leak into creeks and drainage add to the **basura → baha** chain, worsening flood exposure in an already flood-prone Metro Manila. |
| **Economic** | Value is destroyed — households and student orgs throw away money, and landfill tipping and hauling costs are borne publicly. Informal collectors lose steady, sorted supply. |
| **Social / Justice** | Flood and dumpsite burden falls hardest on low-income barangays downstream, not on the campus that generated the waste. Informal waste workers carry the labor and the health risk while holding the least price power. |
| **Political / Institutional** | Landfill capacity keeps being consumed and RA 9003 compliance stays on paper, which pushes LGUs toward expensive end-of-pipe fixes instead of diversion — and weakens the credibility of the waste-sector commitments in our NDC. |
| **Technological** | Recovery infrastructure stays under-used and under-invested: no data on what is actually generated or diverted, so no one can size an MRF, a route, or a program correctly. |

---

## THE SOLUTION (our answer, one line)

**Make the price and the path visible at the moment of disposal, then log the kilograms.**

Operationally, that is **Gargar**: see the scrap price → reach a verified collector →
log the diversion, with an exportable kg total so the impact is counted rather than
claimed.

It attacks **R1** (price opacity) and **R2** (broken last link) directly, and creates the
missing feedback loop for **R4** by making diversion measurable.

> instead of asserted. We're not claiming carbon credits and we're not solving flood
> control. We're claiming kilograms we can show you — and a habit that survives after

## Non-claims (protects us in Q&A)

- We are **not** claiming carbon credits, offsets, or "plastic neutrality."
- We are **not** claiming a flood-control fix — drainage is a **co-benefit**, stated as one.
- We are **not** claiming any collector is verified until someone on our team has
  actually contacted them.
- Any kg figure we present is either **logged** or explicitly labeled a **demo seed**.
