## 1. The three circle

Draw three overlapping circle and answer where they meet:

- **Joy** — what you actually love doing (not what sounds impressive).
- **Strength** — what you are good at, proven by things you have already done.
- **The issue** — the climate problem that keeps bothering you.

Your climate action lives in the center: big enough to matter, personal enough to sustain.

## 2. Prompt (three per circle — answer short, one line each)

| Joy | Strength | The issue |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| When did I last lose track of time helping someone? | What do people ask me to help with? | Which local problem do I keep noticing? |
| What part of YCLAP week do I look forward to? | What have I finished that others started? | Who suffers most from it in our node? |
| What would I do even if nobody clapped? | What tool, habit, or skill comes easy to me? | What gets worse if nobody acts this year? |

## 3. One-sentence statement

Template — fill all four blank:

> Because I love ___, and I'm good at ___, I will ___ about ___.

Filled example from cohort reality:

1. *Gargar diversion logging:* "Because I love turning cleanups into proof, and I'm good at keeping a simple record, I will log every kilo our node diverts in the Gargar diversion log about recyclables that never reach a Pasig junkshop."
2. *Flood-drive mobilizing:* "Because I love rallying neighbors after a storm, and I'm good at organizing people fast, I will mobilize the flood-activated collection drive when PAGASA raises an Orange rainfall warning about flood-borne basura clogging our waterways."
