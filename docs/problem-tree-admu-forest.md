## TRUNK — the problem

**Climate stress on Ateneo’s urban forest: students walk under a green campus they cannot name, while the canopy that cools Loyola Heights is treated as background instead of a shared responsibility.**

One sentence for the slide:

> *Two-thirds of this campus is still green, but most of us cannot name what we are walking under — and a website cannot plant more native trees.*

Why this is the locked problem (26 Aug pulong; Ma’am Ana: stay on the first-slide tree):
- It is **on this campus** — Loyola Heights, not a mangrove or a second recycling app.
- It is **climate** — urban canopy is heat and flood infrastructure, not landscaping.
- It is **ours to form in two weeks.** We cannot increase native-tree representation by 12 Sep. We can form awareness and commitment on *this* walk.

## ROOTS — causes (why this problem is in place)

Word-doc roots. Three, not a fourth we invented.

| # | Root | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 · Infrastructure** | Walkable ground and closed ground are not the same map | A lot of Ateneo forest is off-limits (SOM / swamp patches, fenced groves). Students cannot “baseline” what they are not allowed to enter. AIS already geo-tagged **1,809** trees (SY 2025–2026); the gap is a map students can use on a path, not another inventory. Pedestrian graph work exists (ADMUNAV — Lagyo, Galicia, Guico 2025) and has **not** been shared with this team. |
| **R2 · Policy** | Admin buy-in for campus sustainability is a known struggle, not a missing slogan | Cuyegkeng & Favis (2019) already described how this campus struggles to get administrative buy-in for sustainability programs. Sophie’s v1 “20 representatives by Q3” was demoted on the call. We have **not** consulted AIS, Manila Observatory, CFMO/TAW, or Ateneo Wild/AGILA. Claiming we have is a lie. |
| **R3 · Awareness** | Green is scenery; species are unnamed | Formation was the replacement Change (`32:58`). People walk Gonzaga / CTC / the Library lawn without a common name or a scientific name. Incentives and a public leaderboard were refused (`20:20`). Noticing is the habit; points are not.

**Deepest root (if asked “why?” a fourth time):** the forest is counted by AIS and walked by students, but the two never meet on a surface a phone can open.

## BRANCHES — consequences (what happens if this is not addressed)

Word-doc branches. Four, not five.

| Branch | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Comfort** | Canopy is shade people feel. Urban-canopy / LULC models for Metro Manila (Bilang et al. 2022; Llorin, Villarin et al. 2024) show land cover changes the heat you feel — that is not a campus thermometer we invented. Lose the walkable canopy and the cool-island story of Loyola Heights thins out. |
| **Biodiversity** | Native and endemic trees (Narra, Molave, Katmon) sit next to fast exotic shade (Mahogany, rain tree, teak). Natives often fail underneath plantation canopies — analog Ortiz et al. 2024 (Chile; Manila Observatory), not an Ateneo mahogany survey. PH reforestation already fails when soil and native vs exotic are ignored (Navarrete et al. 2018). Students who cannot name a tree cannot notice the swap. |
| **Cost** | Replacing mature shade after it is lost is slower and more expensive than keeping it. A planting drive as the flagship is rejected for this reason — and because this team cannot claim AIS’s 1,809 as “our baseline.” |
| **Metro green** | Ateneo’s 89 ha (~⅔ still green, AIS) is a cool island inside a hot city. If the campus forest is only a private backdrop, it does not teach other campuses how to copy a walkable-path map, a curated list, a personal journal, and an off-limits geofence. AUN ecological-education networks (Delocado, Tuaño, Lacdao-Umali 2025) are a carrier, not a second app. |

## THE SOLUTION (what a website can actually do)

**Form students to notice and commit to campus biodiversity on this walk — a field guide, not a planting campaign.**

Operationally, that is the Field Guide PWA:

1. A map of **walkable** paths with encounter discs (dummy paths until ADMUNAV / AIS share a file).
2. A **restricted-forest hatch** with no discs inside: “Observed from the path — this grove is off-limits.”
3. A **personal journal** on the phone (localStorage). Reflection, not a race (Rodrigo, Favis, Cuyegkeng 2021 — RECIPE).
4. A **plan** page that lists who we still need to talk to, each row unanswered.

It attacks **R3** (awareness) directly, names **R2** (policy) as unanswered instead of faking a consult, and respects **R1** (infrastructure) by not spawning encounters in closed groves.
