## Spoken draft (≈450 words · practice to ~3:00)

**Hook (20s)**  
Recyclables already have a price in Pasig — aluminum cans, PET, carton. Informal collectors and junkshops already move them. **But** most students and households never see a transparent ₱-per-kilo rate, and there is no clear path from dorm bag to verified shop. **Therefore** we built **Gargar**: see the scrap price, reach a collector, log the diversion.

**Problem (30s)**  
Metro Manila still pushes roughly ten thousand tons of waste a day into stressed landfills — Rodriguez, San Mateo, Navotas. That residual stream is a methane and capacity problem. And flood managers already say garbage clogging drains is a top co-driver of Metro floods — DPWH has cited roughly seventy percent of NCR internal drainage silted with basura and other material. We are not claiming one app fixes floods. We are claiming **opacity and friction** keep diversion low when the scrap market already exists.

**Solution (25s)**  
Gargar is a Pasig pilot: **reference rates**, a **calculator**, a **collector directory** from EcoWaste field data, and a **diversion log** that counts kilograms. Climate stack is honest: waste diversion as mitigation co-benefit, drainage as adaptation co-benefit, and **price transparency as climate justice** for informal recovery workers. SDGs twelve, thirteen, eleven.

**Demo (60s)**  
*[Live: rate board → PET clean → set kg → estimated ₱ → Find a collector → showcase trio → Log diversion.]*  
One happy path. Showcase shops first: Pineda MRF, ACF, Ghie — directory labels until we field-verify. Pilot board shows entries, kilograms toward one hundred, and estimated recovery. Demo seeds are marked so mentors see real versus practice.

**Traction + limits (20s)**  
We have the product loop shipping, rates frozen as **reference as of 7 August**, and a target of thirty users, three field-verified collectors, one hundred kilograms by 12 September. **We do not claim** carbon credits, plastic neutrality, or national flood-control reform. Final ₱ is always the shop after weigh-in.

**Ask + close (20s)**  
We need mentor feedback on metric honesty, one campus org drop window, and one junkshop or MRF handoff for ninety days.  
See the scrap price. Reach a collector. Log the diversion. **That’s Gargar.**
