# R47 — Philippine Youth Climate Organizations (2024–2026): Aksyon Klima youth, Youth for Climate / YACAP / FFF PH, campaigns, channels, coalition map

**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** Youth-led and youth-facing climate organizations in the Philippines, 2024–2026; campaigns, public channels, and coalition structure  
**Method:** Secondary synthesis of org sites, UNICEF/NYC/CCC reporting, media, and campaign posts (web-available sources through early Aug 2026)

---

## Key findings

### 1. Naming map: who is who (and what overlaps)

| Brand / label | What it actually is (2024–2026) | Notes |
|---------------|----------------------------------|--------|
| **YACAP** (Youth Advocates for Climate Action Philippines) | Nationwide **youth alliance**; self-describes as **Fridays for Future Philippines** | Dominant street/justice-oriented youth climate formation. Founded ~2019. |
| **Fridays for Future PH / FFF PH** | **Same ecosystem as YACAP** (not a separate mass org) | Instagram tags YACAP as “#FridaysForFuture PH.” Mitzi Jonelle Tan long-time convenor/international spokesperson; Rae Junsay cited as National Coordinator (2025 media). |
| **Youth for Climate PH / Youth 4 Climate / Youth for Climate Action** | **Not one single legal brand** | Historical and media labels often point to **YS4C PH** and/or **YACAP**. Use carefully; verify handle. |
| **Youth Strike 4 Climate Philippines (YS4C PH)** | Distinct **youth-led** org + campaign (est. May 2019) | Pioneer strike-era formation; storytelling + national demands; parallel to, not identical with, YACAP. |
| **Aksyon Klima Pilipinas (AKP)** | **CSO coordination network** (~49 member orgs), **not** a youth-only org | Youth embedded via leadership (e.g. National Coordinator John Leo Algo, youth-activist background), youth-adjacent members (e.g. Negrosanon Young Leaders Institute), and joint youth events (ASEAN Youth in Climate Action). “Aksyon Klima youth” = participation strand, **not** a formal autonomous wing with its own brand. |
| **Y4JT** (Youth for Just Transition) | Multi-sector **youth network** (FES Philippines–seeded) | Students, young workers, queer advocates, IP youth, media; convened **LCOY PH** in 2023–2024 cycle. |
| **LCOY Philippines** | Annual **UN/YOUNGO-linked** Local Conference of Youth | 2024: Y4JT + NYC; 2025: Positive Youth Development Network + UNICEF + NYC + Kabataang Resilient Network → **National Youth Statement** for COP track. |
| **Climate Reality Project Philippines** | National chapter of Al Gore’s Climate Reality network | Capacity programs: **Klima Eskwela**, **REalTalk Youth** (community RE), COP Youth Fellows. |
| **WWF-Philippines National Youth Council (NYC)** | Institutional youth arm of WWF-PH | Batch 4 (2024–2026); Earth Day, conservation, climate justice advocacy; 10th anniversary arc into 2026. |

**Bottom line:** For outreach and partnership, treat **YACAP ≈ FFF PH** as the main radical-justice youth climate alliance; **YS4C PH** as a second independent pioneer youth org; **AKP** as the multi-org CSO hub that *includes* youth; and **LCOY/Y4JT/UNICEF–NYC track** as the formal statement-and-policy pipeline.

### 2. Core organizations (profiles, 2024–2026)

#### 2.1 YACAP / Fridays for Future Philippines

- **Identity:** Alliance of youth organizations, student councils, and individuals for **climate justice**, urgency, defense of environment defenders, youth-led collective action, and “system change, not climate change.”
- **Structure:** National coordination + **regional chapters** (e.g. **YACAP NCR** active on partner calls, exhibits, mobilizations).
- **Leadership signals:** Mitzi Jonelle Tan (convenor / international spokesperson / FFF international organizer); Rae Junsay (National Coordinator, cited 2025); national external coordination roles appear in public events (e.g. climate-justice panels with CCNCI).
- **Tactics (2024–2026):** Global Climate Strike participation; **Fashion Against Fascism and Fossil Fuels** runway-as-protest (2024); Climate Cafés; podcast; **Youth Climate Agenda** / green-leadership electoral framing; art exhibits (e.g. NCR “Weaving the Waves”); joint actions with fisherfolk (Navotas); policy briefs (e.g. climate issues in Navotas); **National Climate Justice Report 2024**; regular statements linking climate to corruption, labor, LGBTQ+ rural justice, human rights, anti-reclamation/mining, anti–destructive infrastructure.
- **2026 street pulse:** World Environment Day (5–6 Jun 2026) climate strike, Liwasang Bonifacio + silent protest on Quirino Ave over **SALEX** tree-cutting / displacement; co-presence with Saribuhay, Agham Youth, CEC, Panatang Luntian, Kalikasan PNE, Greenpeace PH, student councils, etc. Labor Day 2026 statement: climate advocates “unite with workers” (wage, prices, war frame under Marcos Jr. administration critique).
- **Political character:** Explicitly **intersectional and anti-imperialist** in public framing; high risk environment (red-tagging, police violence statements). Stronger on *justice + protest + culture* than on technocratic implementation.

#### 2.2 Youth Strike 4 Climate Philippines (YS4C PH)

- **Identity:** Pioneer youth-led movement (May 2019); dual identity as **national organization** and **campaign**.
- **Framing:** “Humanize climate action through **activism and storytelling**.”
- **Six national demands (public site):** (1) declare climate emergency; (2) just transition to 100% RE; (3) strengthen adaptation & resilience; (4) phaseout of all fossil fuels; (5) safeguard IPs & environment defenders’ rights; (6) amplify genuine local climate action.
- **Programs:** Young Climate Leaders (YCL) program (climate knowledge + leadership skills); multi-year project portfolio on org site; SIKLAB-type recap events celebrating youth climate activism history.
- **Position vs YACAP:** Overlapping goals and strike lineage; **organizationally distinct**. Both can appear in global strike ecosystems; do not merge brands in CRM or outreach without consent.

#### 2.3 Aksyon Klima Pilipinas (AKP) — youth interface

- **Identity:** PH’s leading **civil society coordination network** on climate (since ~2007); **~49** member CSOs/people’s orgs + individual members (2026 site list).
- **Vision/mission:** Low-carbon, climate-resilient, justice-grounded PH; science-based, inclusive policy at national and UNFCCC levels.
- **Youth angle:** Not a youth membership brand. Youth relevance comes from: (a) **National Coordinator John Leo Algo** (appointed ~2023; youth/environment advocate; media face on climate finance access, PDP climate strategy, COP-related CSO voice); (b) members with youth focus (e.g. **Negrosanon Young Leaders Institute**); (c) large-NGO members that run youth programs (Plan International, Save the Children, World Vision, Greenpeace, WWF-PH, Living Laudato Si’, etc.); (d) participation in **ASEAN Youth in Climate Action** spaces (e.g. Feb 2025 activity at 3ZERO / Komunidad Hub).
- **Work mode:** Government engagement (CCC, NEDA/DEPDev, DOE, DENR, etc.), policy review, UNFCCC advocacy, media on loss & damage/finance accessibility. Best treated as **adult CSO coalition with youth co-ownership**, not as peer youth org.

#### 2.4 Youth for Just Transition (Y4JT) Network

- **Backbone:** Seeded by **Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung (FES) Philippines**; multi-sector youth (students, young professionals, trade union youth, queer advocates, organizers, climate advocates, IP youth, media).
- **Focus:** **Just transition** as intersectional agenda (labor, energy, transport, journalism, IP rights)—not only school strikes.
- **Key delivery:** Just Transition Youth Camps; youth agenda / COP messaging; journalist fellowship with Climate Tracker Asia; **host/organizer of LCOY Philippines 2023–2024** with National Youth Commission support; Philippine Youth Statement on Climate Change **2024** pipeline (consultations → COP29-facing position including **75% RE by 2030 / 100% by 2040** framing cited in public posts).
- **Public channel:** Facebook `@Y4JTnetwork`.

#### 2.5 LCOY Philippines track (2024–2025) — statement factory

| Year | Convening pattern | Output |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| **2024** | Y4JT Network + National Youth Commission (and partners) | **Philippine Youth Statement on Climate Change 2024** (~200+ young leaders incl. SK; NYC adoption; COP29 finance/justice asks; RE targets widely cited) |
| **2025** | Positive Youth Development Network + UNICEF PH + NYC + **Kabataang Resilient Network**; regional LCOYs Luzon–Visayas–Mindanao; national convening Quezon City **11 Oct 2025** (~120 youth from 1,300+ applicants) | **National Youth Statement on Climate Action** — **12-point** recommendations; government + UNICEF backing for **COP30**; themes include RE law implementation, community energy, FPIC, SK seat in local DRRM via BDCs, budgets, accountability |

- LCOY PH is the **YOUNGO-recognized** national youth climate conference path toward GCOY/COP. Separate from YACAP’s protest brand but overlapping participant pool.

#### 2.6 Climate Reality Project Philippines

- **Mode:** Training, climate literacy, leadership corps alumni, youth fellowships to COPs.
- **Flagship youth programs 2024–2026:** **Klima Eskwela** (multi-leg school/community climate science–arts–action; e.g. Butuan 2025, Pangasinan State University, etc.); **REalTalk Youth** (e.g. Jul 2025 and Jul 2026 workshops on community-based renewable energy program design).
- **Tone:** Capacity-building and solution design; closer to mainstream/institutional climate education than YACAP’s system-change street politics.

#### 2.7 WWF-Philippines National Youth Council

- **Mode:** Select cohort of youth leaders under WWF-PH brand.
- **Batch 4 (2024–2026):** Ricky Asuncion, Javie Barcinal, Ron Caguliodo, Dani de Leon, Renz Luyao, Shri Macaumbao, Mirus Ponon (public roster).
- **Activity:** Earth Day 2025 (Navotas, “Building Connections”); cleanups, art, Map of Youth Action, climate justice advocacy; 2026 **10th anniversary** narrative and community-led conservation stories.
- **Alumni bridge:** e.g. Deign Frolley Soriano (Batch 1 alum) writing publicly on ASEAN Youth Declaration / COP track and youth tokenism barriers (Inquirer, mid-2025).

### 5. Cross-cutting dynamics (2024–2026)

1. **Two-lane movement:** (A) **Street/justice** lane (YACAP/FFF, Agham Youth, Kalikasan, etc.) vs (B) **Institutional/statement** lane (LCOY, NYC, UNICEF, CCC National Day, Climate Reality trainings, WWF NYC). Same generation; different risk tolerance and theory of change.
2. **Just transition as shared language:** Y4JT, YACAP consultations, AKP policy work, and National Youth Statements all use JT—but with different emphases (labor/IP/FPIC vs RE targets vs finance).
3. **Intersectional branding is non-negotiable** for the YACAP lane: climate without queer, labor, anti-corruption, anti-imperial, and defender justice frames is seen as incomplete (“There is no climate justice without ____ justice”).
4. **Security and civic space:** PH remains high-risk for environment defenders; youth groups issue frequent solidarity and anti-brutality statements—partnership design must account for this.
5. **Tokenism critique:** Youth repeatedly report being platformed at COP/high-level events without power over final decisions (publicly argued by WWF NYC alumni and LCOY messaging: “We are resilient — but we demand more”).
6. **State calendar hooks:** 25 November National Day for Youth in Climate Action; Environment Month (June); Earth Day; COP prep windows; ASEAN youth climate days—predictable amplification moments.
7. **No single “Youth for Climate PH” monopoly:** Brand collisions exist historically; **always confirm legal/org identity before attributing quotes or MOUs**.

### Coalition typology (practical)

| Layer | Who | Best use |
|-------|-----|----------|
| **A. Mass youth justice alliance** | YACAP/FFF PH + campus allies | Mobilization, narrative, campus density |
| **B. Pioneer storytelling org** | YS4C PH | Campaign collab, youth leadership pipeline |
| **C. Multi-CSO climate hub** | Aksyon Klima Pilipinas | Policy, multi-org legitimacy, finance/L&D |
| **D. Just transition network** | Y4JT (FES-linked) | Cross-sector youth (labor–energy–media) |
| **E. Statement / COP pipeline** | LCOY → NYC/UNICEF/CCC | Formal youth positions to government & COP |
| **F. Training / conservation brands** | Climate Reality PH, WWF NYC | Workshops, RE literacy, conservation prestige |
| **G. People’s environment front** | Kalikasan, CEC, Agham, Panatang Luntian | Joint actions on mining, reclamation, defenders |

**Overlap rule:** Individuals often hold **multiple affiliations** (e.g. historically Agham Youth + YACAP; AKP coordinator with faith-based/Living Laudato Si’ ties). Coalitions are **networked**, not siloed memberships.
