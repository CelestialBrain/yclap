# R50 — Student / youth scrap-recycling marketplaces in Asia (ID · IN · VN · PH) vs Gargar

**Wave:** 3 agent deep research  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** Household ↔ collector / scrap-rate / diversion products with **student, school, campus, or youth** traction in **Indonesia, India, Vietnam, Philippines** — comparable to **Gargar** (reference ₱/kg + verified Pasig collector path + diversion log). Not B2B industrial scrap boards or pure EPR credit ledgers.  
**Pilot lens:** What features and operating patterns fit a **5-week Youth CLAP pilot** (≥30 users, ≥3 field-verified collectors, ≥100 kg diverted or honest proxy).

---

## Key findings

### 1. The category is real — and almost nobody is “student-only”

Across the four markets, the winning pattern is **general household scrap UX** that **youth adopt first** (campus density, social proof, phones, small volumes), not a closed student marketplace.

| Country | Closest Gargar analogues | Youth / school angle | Core job |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------------|----------|
| **Indonesia** | **Rapel**, **Octopus**, **Duitin**, **Kepul**; waste-bank (*bank sampah*) digitization; **Plastic Bank Schools** | University users post sorted kg (e.g. Rapel Yogya student ~25 kg/post); *pelestari* include students; school waste banks + P5/Adiwiyata curriculum hooks | Post sorted scrap → pickup / drop → cash or e-wallet points |
| **India** | **ScrapUncle** (student-founded), **The Kabadiwala**, **Recykal** (B2B layer + campus offtake) | ScrapUncle started as IIIT-Delhi student product (2019); college/office bulk pickups common | See rate list → book doorstep kabadi → digital weigh + instant pay |
| **Vietnam** | **VECA** (closest product twin), **mGreen**, **Grac** marketplace | Urban youth + parents; school “green points” / carton collection campaigns; informal *ve chai* professionalization | App shows ₫/kg + book informal collector; optional points |
| **Philippines** | **Plastic Bank Schools**, **Rezbin**, **TrashCash → Circulo+**, **Trash Panda** (see also R10) | DepEd WinS alignment; Valenzuela and other district onboarding; campus/mall drives | Points or Social Plastic tokens + drop / branch; rarely open junkshop rate board |

**Structural fact (all four):** Informal collectors and junkshops already *are* the scrap marketplace. Apps succeed when they **digitize discovery, price, and proof** — not when they replace junkshops with a pure C2C listing board.

### 2. Country deep dives (features · metrics · what stuck)

#### A. Indonesia — apps + waste banks + school plastic programs

| Product | Model & features | Scale / metrics (public, various years) | Lesson for Gargar |
|---------|------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Rapel** (*Rakyat Peduli Lingkungan*) | Door-to-door inorganic scrap; multi-material (plastic, paper, metal, e-waste, used oil); **price list updated ~biweekly**; users post sorted waste; **24h pickup SLA**; collector uniforms; Rapel balance → **Gopay**; partner hotels/cafes | Yogya + Central Java cities; ~**40 t/month** inorganic at one 2021 snapshot; avg post ~**15 kg**; ~**IDR 1,200/kg** blended balance example; student users document ~**25 kg** posts + social invite loops | **Hyperlocal + SLA + collector dignity (uniform)** beats national ambition. Youth are supply ambassadors, not only end-users |
| **Octopus** | Consumer app + **pelestari** (rebranded pickers — includes students/gig workers); checkpoints; sorting facilities; brand/ESG reporting; e-wallet/points path | 2022: **$5M** raise; claimed **150k** monthly users, **60k+** pelestari, **20+** brands; earlier: **300+ t** plastic / 6 months in 3 regions; later marketing: multi-city, thousands of collectors | End-to-end logistics + brand offtake is **beyond 5-week pilot**. Copy **naming justice** (*pelestari*) and collector onboarding, not CapEx |
| **Duitin** | Collection + recycling platform; digitizes deposits/transactions/routes; waste-bank style rural–urban; IKEA and UNDP/EPPIC-linked activations | ADB plastics-digital pilot shortlist (ID); continuous brand/community drop zones | **Ledger of kg + route** is the product; physical node partners matter more than AI |
| **EkoLogis** | Recyclables **marketplace for informal collectors** (buyer transparency) | ADB digital shortlist (ID) | B2 collector transparency complements household rate tools — Gargar’s justice side |
| **Kepul** (Medan-origin) | Sell household recyclables via app → digital balance/cash; transparent pricing narrative | Regional North Sumatra footprint | Same job as Rapel at smaller geo |
| **PlasticPay / drop-box models** | Bottles → credits at malls/schools | Schools as deposit nodes | Drop infrastructure ≠ price transparency |
| **Bank sampah (national system)** | Community “waste bank”: deposit recyclables, save credit, redeem goods/fees; schools & students long-standing cadres | KLHK order-of-magnitude **>11,000** waste banks nationally (system claim) | **School as aggregation account** works without a startup; Gargar can treat org/dorm as “micro waste bank” with digital log |

**Indonesia pattern:** Sort → post → pickup within hours → cash/points; **price cadence** (e.g. 2 weeks) is explicit; **collector rebranding + uniform** unlocks gated housing; university students are documented early adopters and recruiters.

#### B. India — “digital kabadiwala” with public rate lists

| Product | Model & features | Scale / metrics | Lesson for Gargar |
|---------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **ScrapUncle** (ex-Swapeco) | Founded **2019 by Mukul Chhabra while BTech (IIIT-Delhi)** after kabadi opacity pain; schedule pickup; **ISO digital scales**; **rates regularly updated in-app**; multi-category (paper, plastic, metal, e-waste, appliances); instant digital pay | Delhi-NCR core; marketing claims **hundreds of thousands of users**; **₹22 Cr** pre-Series A reported 2025–26; founder narrative “kabad → crore” growth story | **Student founder origin + transparent rate list + verified crew + weigh proof** is the closest **founder myth** twin to a youth climate pilot |
| **The Kabadiwala** | Doorstep scrap for **40+** categories; multi-city; **transparent pricing / real-time rates**; cash/UPI; impact metrics (water/energy savings framing) | Founded 2013 (Bhopal); **~$3.3M** funding (Tracxn-class figures); **15 major cities** marketed; large pickup/kg claims in PR | Mature ops prove **rate board + trusted pickup** is the durable household job — still **ops-heavy** |
| **Recykal** | Managed **B2B** circular marketplace (plastic, paper, metal, e-waste); campus/institutional offtake as supply | 30+ states/UTs marketplace narrative; Google Sustainability case | Too heavy for pilot; useful as **downstream offtake** story only |

**India pattern:** Lead with **“see today’s rate”**, then **book → weigh → pay**. Students/offices are dense supply; pure marketplace without logistics failed historically — winners own or tightly control the crew. **Do not** promise ScrapUncle-grade logistics in 5 weeks.

#### C. Vietnam — VECA as product twin; mGreen as points twin

| Product | Model & features | Scale / metrics | Lesson for Gargar |
|---------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **VECA** | First major VN app linking households to informal *ve chai* collectors; **homepage rate board** (paper grades, iron, PET, aluminum, Tetra Pak, etc.); book sale (weekday/weekend windows); cash or **MoMo**; points section; **no chat in-app** (off-app arrange); later **junkshop owner app** for invoices / formalization / EPR traceability (USAID Reducing Pollution) | **19 districts** HCMC; **~43,000 users**; **~120 tons** recyclables **in 2023 alone** (Winrock/USAID); City Without Trash prize **EUR 10k**; young collectors (e.g. 22-yo full-time) enter via app; training on grading | **Closest UI twin to Gargar:** price table + collector path + informal sector. Lessons: (1) **simplify UI** after year-1 complexity; (2) **time-window vagueness** hurts trust; (3) collectors may **lose money** without offtake skill — train or verify resale path; (4) **zero fee marketplace** needs another monetization (EPR/shop formalization); (5) junkshop digitization is phase-2 |
| **mGreen** | Segregation education + **reward points** redeemable with local partners; bin-full request / collection cues | Founded ~2018 social enterprise; ADB shortlist; Hue and city pilots | Points work when **partner density** is real; weak without redemption grid |
| **Grac** | Municipal waste software + **online recycling marketplace** | Municipal + marketplace hybrid | LGU coupling is power — not portable to student pilot alone |
| School carton / green-point programs | SIG “Recycle for Good”, Green Point zero-waste city models | Behavior + thin material streams | Good mobilize layer; not scrap justice |

**Vietnam pattern:** **“Grab for ve chai”** is the pitch. Success metrics stay **local (one metro)** for years. Formalizing **shops** (not only households) is what unlocks EPR-grade kg. Youth collectors appear when UX is phone-native.

#### D. Philippines — school digital deposit > open scrap board

| Product | Model & features | Scale / metrics | Lesson for Gargar |
|---------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Plastic Bank Schools** (ID + PH) | Students bring PET/HDPE/PP (+ other recyclables); school MRF/waste bank; sell to **Plastic Bank branch or preferred junkshop**; **app records every sale** (GPS, facade photo, materials photo, receipt photo, selfie); tokens → **GCash / Gojek**; curriculum hooks (DepEd WinS; Indonesia P5/GSS/Adiwiyata) | SC Johnson partnership: **140+ schools** ID+PH narrative; national Plastic Bank network much larger (branches/members — see R10) | **Best school-proof pattern for Gargar diversion log:** photo + place + kg + receipt proxy. Flexibility to sell to **existing junkshop** is critical |
| **Rezbin** | Smart bin + QR + gamified points; B2B workplace | Metro + Iloilo expansion; hardware CapEx | UX polish yes; **not** pilot CapEx |
| **TrashCash → Circulo+** | AI recognition + points at booths; barangay drives | ~10k+ early Play installs; rebrand | Points without permanent offtake stall |
| **Trash Panda** | On-demand recoverable pickup + coaching | Metro launch era | Unit economics of multi-material last-mile are harsh without subsidy |

**PH pattern:** Brand/EPR and school programs own **traceable kg**; **no national open ₱/kg + junkshop directory** product dominates (R10). That is Gargar’s white space — **if** collectors are field-true.

### 3. Cross-country patterns (worked vs failed)

#### Worked (repeatable)

| Pattern | Where seen | Why it works |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| **Public reference rate board** | ScrapUncle, Kabadiwala, VECA, Rapel | Removes haggling asymmetry; students share screenshots |
| **Verified collector path (not anonymous listing)** | All successful consumer apps | Safety + reliability; uniforms (Rapel) reduce stigma |
| **Physical aggregation node** | Campus, school MRF, waste bank, mall drop, hotel | Thin household kg becomes economic volume |
| **Diversion proof with photos/GPS/receipt** | Plastic Bank Schools | Mentor-safe metrics without full blockchain |
| **Cash / e-wallet sooner than soft points** | India digital kabadi; Rapel→Gopay; VECA cash/MoMo | Points only work with dense redemption partners |
| **Justice rename + training** | Octopus *pelestari*; Rapel uniforms; VECA grading training | Youth climate pitch + worker dignity align |
| **Hyperlocal first** | Rapel Yogya; VECA HCMC districts; ScrapUncle NCR | Liquidity and trust are local |

#### Failed or chronically thin

| Pattern | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **Pure C2C scrap marketplace, no logistics or offtake** | Two-sided liquidity; households already have walk-in junkshops |
| **On-demand multi-material pickup as pure startup margin** | Fuel + labor > scrap value (Trash Panda–class; India winners subsidize with volume/ops) |
| **AI scan as moat** | Buyer acceptance still human; nice education, not offtake |
| **Points without partners** | Churn when redemption is slow |
| **National scale before phone-verified collectors** | Dead numbers destroy trust forever |
| **Ignoring informal resale skill** | VECA young collectors lost money to consolidators early |
| **Student campaigns without permanent drop path** | One-off upcycling weeks don’t compound kg |

### 4. Differentiation map vs Gargar (Asia cohort)

| Dimension | Asia “digital kabadi / ve chai / waste bank app” | **Gargar 5-week pilot wedge** |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Primary job | Book pickup / earn points / brand EPR kg | **See reference ₱/kg + reach verified Pasig collector + log diversion** |
| User | Mass household + brands | **Ateneo / Pasig student + nearby household** |
| Price | In-app live or biweekly rates | **EcoWaste-field reference**, labeled reference, re-verified |
| Logistics | Owned or partner fleet | **Directory + optional campus window** — no fleet |
| Proof | Ops dashboard / EPR certificates | **Diversion log ≥100 kg, ≥30 users, ≥3 in-person collectors** |
| Climate claim | Often plastic / Social Plastic / ESG | **Landfill load + methane + basura→baha** — **no credits** |
| Justice | Variable (some rebrand pickers; some bypass) | **Price transparency + informal path**, not displacement |
| Scope | Multi-city ambition | **One geo that can finish in 5 weeks** |

**Non-goals for pilot (Asia overbuild):** smart bins, national marketplace, brand PRO ledger, AI grading, multi-city pickup SLA.

### 5. Lessons specifically for a **5-week** pilot scope

Prioritize ruthlessly. Asia’s multi-year apps still struggle with collector density and offtake. A Youth CLAP pilot should **simulate the first honest slice**, not clone Octopus or ScrapUncle ops.

| Week focus (suggested) | Borrow from | Ship / do |
|------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| **W1 — Rate truth** | VECA homepage rates; ScrapUncle list; Rapel biweekly update habit | Freeze **reference ₱/kg table** (PET, HDPE, cardboard, aluminum, copper caution) with **“as of / source / not a quote”** |
| **W1–2 — Collector truth** | Rapel uniform trust; VECA verified associates; Plastic Bank junkshop flexibility | **In-person verify ≥3 Pasig collectors** (name, map pin, hours, materials, phone that rings). No “book” CTA on dead numbers |
| **W2–3 — Student funnel** | Rapel student social invite; campus bulk; school eco-champion | One org (BOx/MEcO/etc.) + one **drop window or handoff ritual**; target **30 users who price**, not 30 app installs only |
| **W3–4 — Diversion log** | Plastic Bank Schools photo stack (place + materials + receipt + selfie optional) | Log: user, material, **kg**, destination collector, timestamp, **photo**. Export for showcase |
| **W4–5 — Story + honesty** | Octopus justice language; India founder myth; VECA “simplify UI” | 3-min pitch: transparency + path + kg; **explicit non-claims** (no credits, no national fleet) |

**Minimum feature set that Asia evidence supports for 5 weeks:**

1. **Reference rate board** (static or weekly refresh)  
2. **Calculator** (kg × rate → ₱ estimate)  
3. **Verified collector directory** (3+, field-checked)  
4. **Diversion log** with photo proof  
5. **Optional:** campus drop schedule card (not a booking engine)

**Explicitly out of scope for 5 weeks:** dynamic multi-party marketplace, instant UPI/GCash payout, AI recognition, hardware bins, EPR certificates, multi-city expansion, owned pickup fleet.

**Pilot metric honesty (Asia-calibrated):**  
VECA’s **120 t/year city-wide** and Rapel’s **40 t/month** are multi-year ops. Gargar’s **100 kg** is a **proof-of-flow** metric, not a scale claim — frame as *first verified loop*, not *national diversion*.

### 6. Student / youth design cues worth copying

| Cue | Source | Apply |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Shareable rate screenshot | India apps, VECA | Rates UI must look “postable” in org chats |
| Student as collector/gig *or* supplier | Octopus pelestari; Rapel Yogya student | Ambassadors sort & log; do **not** force students into hazardous picking |
| School account + champion role | Plastic Bank Schools | One org lead owns kg board |
| Off-app arrange after match | VECA | Directory can deep-link SMS/call; full chat is optional |
| Stigma reduction for collectors | Rapel uniforms; Octopus rename | Pitch language: partners, not “scavengers” |
| Social invite loops | TikTok/IG student posts (Rapel) | Mobilize lane, not Build lane |

## Gargar feature shortlist

Ranked for **5-week Youth CLAP pilot** (must → should → later). Informed by Asia analogues above + Gargar canvas (rates · Pasig collectors · diversion log).

### Must ship (pilot-critical)

| # | Feature | Asia inspiration | Why |
|---|---------|------------------|-----|
| 1 | **Reference rate board** (₱/kg by material; last-checked date; EcoWaste/field source) | VECA rate icons; ScrapUncle/Kabadiwala lists; Rapel biweekly updates | Core job; shareable; justice via transparency |
| 2 | **Kg → ₱ calculator** (estimate only, disclaimer) | All India digital kabadi flows | Converts curiosity → action |
| 3 | **Verified collector directory** (≥3 Pasig; pin, hours, materials, working phone) | VECA associates; Rapel partners; Plastic Bank “preferred junkshop” flexibility | Path beats points theater |
| 4 | **Diversion log** (user, material, kg, destination, time) | Plastic Bank Schools transaction record; Duitin deposit ledger | Showcase metric; ≥100 kg proof |
| 5 | **Photo proof attachment** on log entry (materials and/or receipt) | Plastic Bank GPS/facade/receipt/selfie stack (simplify to 1–2 photos) | Mentor-safe honesty without ERP |

### Should ship if bandwidth

| # | Feature | Asia inspiration | Why |
|---|---------|------------------|-----|
| 6 | **Campus / org drop window card** (date, place, collector present) | School MRF; waste bank day; hotel/cafe bulk (Rapel B2B) | Aggregates thin student kg |
| 7 | **Material ID cheat sheet** (what is PET vs “lata” vs aluminum) | VECA category explainers; India grade education | Cuts contamination and wrong-rate errors |
| 8 | **Collector verification badge** (“visited YYYY-MM-DD”) | Rapel uniform trust signal | Prevents directory rot |
| 9 | **Export CSV / simple kg board** for pitch | Ops dashboards everywhere | Evidence kit |

### Explicitly later (post-showcase / 90-day)

| # | Feature | Why delay |
|---|---------|-----------|
| L1 | In-app booking + SLA timer | Needs live dispatch density (Rapel 24h / ScrapUncle crews) |
| L2 | Instant e-wallet payout | Requires float, KYC, offtake margin |
| L3 | Points / gamification leaderboard | Only after redemption partner exists (mGreen/Rezbin lesson) |
| L4 | AI photo material recognition | Nice; not offtake; Circulo-class distraction |
| L5 | Smart bins / QR stations | Rezbin CapEx |
| L6 | National marketplace / multi-city | Liquidity death; VECA stayed HCMC for years |
| L7 | EPR / plastic-credit product | Integrity risk; YCLAP non-claim |
| L8 | Junkshop owner inventory app | VECA phase-2; valuable justice extension after household loop works |

### Pitch one-liner (Asia-informed)

> *Asia already proved “digital kabadi / ve chai” works when rates are public and collectors are real — Gargar does the Pasig student slice: reference ₱/kg, three verified collectors, and a diversion log you can defend in three minutes.*
