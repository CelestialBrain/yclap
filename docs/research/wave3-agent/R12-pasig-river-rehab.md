## Key findings

### 1. Institutional arc (who owns rehab now)

| Period | Body | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| 1999–Nov 2019 | **Pasig River Rehabilitation Commission (PRRC)** | Single-window rehab: easement recovery, River Warriors, floating barriers, water-quality monitoring toward Class C, estero cleanups, resettlement coordination. Asia Riverprize 2018. |
| Nov 2019 | **PRRC abolished** (EO under Duterte) | Functions split to **Manila Bay Task Force**, **DENR**, **DHSUD**, **MMDA**, **DPWH**. Continuity risk: no single river owner. |
| ~2020 onward | **Pasig River Coordinating and Management Office (PRCMO)** under DENR-NCR | Day-to-day solid-waste and floating-debris ops, River Warriors (~163 environmental aides as of mid-2024), trash boats, barriers, coordination with PCG and LGUs; water-quality analysis MOAs with EMB. |
| Jul 2023 | **IAC-PRUD** (EO 35) | Inter-Agency Council for the Pasig River Urban Development — urban renewal + rehab of banks and nearby water systems. |
| Jan 2024–2026 | **“Pasig Bigyang Buhay Muli” (PBBM)** phases | Flagship: esplanades, mixed-use riverfront, ferry/tourism narrative (Phase 1 esplanade Jan 2024; later phases through 2025–26). **Urban-development heavy**; water chemistry secondary in public messaging. |
| Aug 2025 | **OPAPRR** (EO 92) | Office of the Presidential Adviser on Pasig River Rehabilitation (secretary-rank chair of reorganized IAC-PRUD). **MMDA chair = vice-chair**. Mandate: policy advice, LGU coordination, harmonize programs, bi-annual reports to the President. |

**MMDA** (persistent): floating-garbage ops historically; **Pasig River Ferry** operator (free, except Sundays); vice-chair of IAC-PRUD; co-pilot of tech cleanups (e.g. Clearbot with ADB TA).

**Bottom line:** PRRC is **gone**. Ops live in **PRCMO + MMDA + PCG**; political ownership re-centralized under **OPAPRR / IAC-PRUD** for the PBBM urban-revival frame. Youth/NGO partners should not pitch “partner with PRRC.”

### 2. Water quality (2020–2026): improved in places, not “Class C recovered”

- **Target:** DENR Class C (fishery / recreation / industrial after treatment) — e.g. DO ≥ ~5 mg/L, BOD ≤ ~7 mg/L (criteria as commonly cited for Class C).
- **PRRC-era claim:** partial stations met some Class C parameters before abolition (self-reported milestones; not full-system recovery).
- **2020:** FOI / PRRC-era station data still cited; EMB–PRCMO MOA (Feb 2020) for water-quality analysis of the Pasig system.
- **2025 academic samples (Manila City mouth-area, 3 points):** fecal coliform averaged ~**5.27 × 10⁵ MPN/100 mL**; oil/grease present in all samples; industrial-adjacent sites worst; authors still frame river as largely “biologically dead” / rehab “largely unsuccessful” (IEICES 2025, DLSU).
- **2025 geospatial WQI (UPOU capstone):** CCME-WQI scores ~**30.3–38.9** (avg **~33.9**) → **“poor”** across assessed stations.
- **Manila Bay Rehabilitation Program (2019–early 2024)** overlapped Pasig floodplain landscape change; program close did **not** equal Pasig meeting Class C.

**Honest pitch line:** surface cleanups and esplanades ≠ restored ecology. Wastewater, fecal load, and industrial discharge remain the hard problem; solid-waste diversion is **necessary but not sufficient**.

### 3. Floating litter & plastic load

| Signal | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Global ranking (Meijer et al. / Ocean Cleanup modeling, 2021) | **Pasig often ranked #1 river** for ocean plastic; ~**63,000 t/yr** plastic emission estimate commonly cited; PH rivers ~356,000 t/yr collectively. |
| Manila Bay region (TOC Smart River Survey, 2026 context) | ~**3,500–4,400 t/yr** plastic emissions region-wide (model band); Meycauayan alone ~370–540 t/yr. |
| DENR-PRCMO ops | **Jan–mid-May 2024:** ~**1.6 million kg** mixed solid waste + water hyacinth (~53k sacks) from main stem (Del Pan–San Juan confluence), San Juan River, 25 minor tributaries (Manila/QC), BASECO/Parola; 3 trash boats + barriers + River Warriors. |
| ICTSI / RiverRecycle (San Juan tributary) | River systems collecting on order of **~120 t/month** at peak configurations (project claims); San Juan framed as major plastic feeder into Pasig → Manila Bay. |
| MMDA + ADB Clearbot pilot (late 2025–early 2026) | **Ilugin River (Pasig City):** >7,000 m² water hyacinth ~**138 t** over ~3 months (Oct 2025–Jan 2026). **San Juan River:** **~5.3 t** floating waste in 2 months. |
| The Ocean Cleanup × DENR (4 Jun 2026) | **5-year** agreement: Interceptor tech + data for Pasig / Manila Bay Region under **30 Cities Program**; knowledge transfer to **PRCMO**; first PH barrier rollout targeted **Meycauayan** (Bulacan) mid-2026; Pasig sites under PRCMO identification. Complements **RA 11898 EPR** narrative (upstream producer responsibility + midstream intercept). |
| 2026 ops continuity | DENR-NCR / PRCMO regular cleanups still documented (e.g. Intramuros esplanade floating debris, Jun 2026). |

**Interpretation:** Extraction capacity is **scaling** (boats, barriers, AI vessels, Interceptors), but load is still **tonnage-class**. End-of-pipe alone will not outrun household residual + poorly managed SWM. **Upstream diversion of recyclables** is the complementary lever Gargar can claim honestly.

### 4. Youth & volunteer cleanups

- **River Warriors:** PRRC-era volunteer/livelihood model; post-PRRC, professionalized as PRCMO “environmental aides” (~163 in 2024) doing daily bank/floating collection — not primarily weekend student brigades.
- **Kapit Bisig para sa Ilog Pasig (KBPIP / ABS-CBN Foundation lineage):** historical youth/school estero cleanups (Paco, Pandacan, etc.); brand still referenced in older materials; not the current primary operational spine.
- **Student / corporate / church cleanups:** continue ad hoc with LGUs, DENR, IA (Intramuros), PCG — high visibility, low structural tonnage vs professional trash boats.
- **Volunteer role in 2026 policy climate:** useful for **IEC, community legitimacy, and pilot recruitment**; not a substitute for SWM, sewerage, or Interceptor fleets.

### 5. Geography for a Pasig scrap pilot

| Node | Why it matters for Gargar |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Pasig City (Ilugin & esteros)** | Documented floating-litter / hyacinth problem; MMDA tech pilot already ran here — LGU/MMDA language of “clean waterways” is live. |
| **San Juan River corridor** | Repeatedly named as major solid-waste feeder into Pasig; intercept + circular projects already active. |
| **Main stem (Manila esplanade zones)** | High political visibility (PBBM, OPAPRR); good for storytelling, weak for exclusive pilot ownership (crowded agency space). |
| **Marikina / Taguig-Pateros tributaries** | PRCMO-monitored major tributaries; flood–waste narrative strong. |
| **Ateneo–Katipunan / QC creeks** | Upstream of or adjacent to drainage that loads Manila Bay system; campus pilot + one Pasig/QC external node matches YCLAP geography freeze. |
| **Junkshop belt (Pasig LGU)** | Aligns with EcoWaste directory + Gargar collector path — diversion happens **before** material becomes floating litter. |

**Basura → baha:** solid waste clogging drainage remains a documented co-driver of urban flood aggravation; river rehab messaging and Gargar climate framing can share this line **without** claiming flood-control engineering impact.

## YCLAP-Gargar implication

1. **Frame correctly.** Gargar is **upstream circular diversion** (rates + collector path + kg log), not a river-cleanup app and not a water-quality product. Position as **complement** to PRCMO/MMDA/TOC intercept — “less trash enters the river because it had a market path first.”

2. **Agency language (pitch-safe).** Say **DENR-PRCMO**, **MMDA**, **IAC-PRUD / OPAPRR**, **Pasig Bigyang Buhay Muli** — never “we work with PRRC” (abolished 2019). Optional partner adjacent: barangay SK, Pasig CENRO-type units, school orgs — not national Interceptor deployment.

3. **Climate claims that survive scrutiny.**
   - **Do claim:** landfill load / residual reduction; methane co-benefit of organics *if* organics lane added later; **basura → baha** co-benefit of keeping recyclables out of drainage; justice for informal recovery.
   - **Do not claim:** Class C restoration, Manila Bay cleanup attribution, plastic-credit neutrality, or kg-for-kg Ocean Cleanup substitution.

4. **Geography freeze stays smart.** **Ateneo + one Pasig (or QC) node** is defensible: Pasig City has active waterway-clean narrative (Ilugin), scrap market density, and EcoWaste-scrapable junkshops. Prefer **barangay-level collector verification** over riverfront spectacle events.

5. **Volunteer angle for Mobilize lane.** One co-branded cleanup can recruit pilot users and photo evidence, but **hero metric remains kg diverted via Gargar log**, not sacks collected from the river.

6. **Policy tailwind without dependency.** EPR (RA 11898), Manila Bay / Pasig presidential priority, and TOC–DENR 2026 deal keep **plastic + river** in headlines through showcase season — use as **context**, not product dependency.

7. **Evidence kit add-ons (Story lane).** 30-second beat: “63k t plastic model ranking still defines the river’s global reputation; PRCMO pulled 1.6M kg in ~4.5 months of 2024 alone; interceptors scale midstream — Gargar shrinks the inflow at household/campus.” Cite Ocean Cleanup modeling + Philstar/PRCMO 2024 ops; flag model vs measured uncertainty.
