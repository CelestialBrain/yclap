# R39 — 3-minute climate pitch structure that works for judges

**Scope:** Youth CLAP / Innovation Showcase style student climate pitches (not VC fundraise).  
**Flagship application:** Gargar pilot (rates + Pasig collectors + diversion log).  
**Date:** 2026-08-07

---

## Key findings

### What judges actually score in ~3 minutes

Judges in short student climate / innovation showcases cannot diligence a company. They score **clarity, credibility, and care** under time pressure:

1. **Do I understand the problem in one breath?** Local, human, quantified — not “climate change is bad.”
2. **Is there a real intervention, not a slogan?** Show the thing (demo, photo of pilot, log entry).
3. **Is impact claimed honestly?** Measured kg / users / partners beat speculative tCO₂e.
4. **Who is this for, and why them first?** “Everyone” fails; a named segment wins.
5. **What happens next if we win your attention?** A crisp ask (feedback, partner, next 90 days) beats a vague “support us.”

For climate specifically: **impact is a multiplier, not a substitute for a working loop.** Lead with the user pain and the pilot proof; attach climate (methane diversion, drainage co-benefit, justice) as the *why it matters*, not the only *why it exists*. Mentors and climate judges still punish carbon-credit cosplay and unfalsifiable planetary claims.

### Hooks that work (first 15–25 seconds)

Judges form competence/trust impressions within ~30 seconds (delivery + content). The opener must do three jobs: **attention, orientation, stakes.**

| Hook type | Pattern | When it works | Risk |
|-----------|---------|---------------|------|
| **Startling local fact** | One number + place | Waste, flood, heat pitches with real data | Global stats that feel borrowed |
| **Lived scenario** | “Last rainy season, basura clogged…” | Adaptation / justice angles | Melodrama without a next beat |
| **Contradiction** | “Recyclables have value — households still can’t see the price” | Market-failure framing (Gargar) | Cleverness without clarity |
| **Character** | One collector / student / household | Justice + human scale | Tokenism if they never reappear |
| **Action prompt** | “Picture sorting a week of PET and not knowing the ₱ rate” | Immersion | Overused “imagine if…” fluff |

**Prefer:** question *or* startling fact *or* character — then **name the offer in ≤8–12 words** (mission line).  
**Avoid as openers:** team intros, long org backstory, “Hi I’m from…”, reading slide titles, rhetorical questions with no answer in the next line.

ABT story spine (3MT / SciComm standard) maps cleanly onto climate pitches:

> **And** (shared world) → **But** (tension) → **Therefore** (your intervention + path)

Example spine for Gargar:  
*Recyclables have market value **and** informal collectors already recover materials — **but** households and students can’t see transparent rates or a clear path to a collector — **therefore** Gargar puts rates, a Pasig directory, and a diversion log in one flow so diversion becomes measurable.*

### Problem framing (climate + justice, mentor-safe)

Strong problem slides / segments follow: **hook → one pain → one metric → who feels it → urgency (local).**

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| One urgent pain point (opacity + friction → low diversion) | Kitchen-sink climate crisis |
| Quantify *local* impact (kg residual, clogged drainage, missed ₱) | Only global tCO₂e or “billions of market” |
| Name who is hurt *and* who already works the system (households + informal recovery) | Erase junkshops / collectors as invisible |
| State **non-claims** mentally even if unspoken (no carbon product, no national flood reform) | Imply plastic neutrality / offset magic |
| Climate layer as stack: mitigation (methane) + adaptation co-benefit (drainage) + justice (fair path / transparency) | Lead with “we save the planet” |

**Pitch the problem before the product.** Audiences unfamiliar with waste markets cannot evaluate Gargar until they feel the friction. Feature lists without a felt problem sound like another app.

### Demo (the center of gravity for student climate pitches)

In investor 3-minutes, traction often carries; in **showcase / mentor / student climate** formats, the **live or recorded demo is the truth serum**. Judges trust what they see over what they hear.

**Demo rules for 3 minutes:**

1. **One happy path only** — e.g. rate look-up → collector → log kg. No edge cases.
2. **Narrate the user, not the stack** — “a student prices PET” not “we use React + EcoWaste scrape.”
3. **Freeze one proof number on screen** — users, kg, collectors verified (even if pilot-small).
4. **Backup path** — recorded 30–45s screen capture if Wi‑Fi dies (Story lane AV checklist).
5. **Never demo vapor** — claim freeze before pitch; no “book a collector” if numbers aren’t verified.

Time budget: **45–70 seconds** of visible product is better than 20 seconds of app + 50 seconds of architecture.

### The ask (close that doesn’t fizzle)

Student climate pitches rarely ask for a Series A. They ask for **permission to continue with the room’s help.**

Effective asks are **specific, sized, and milestone-linked**:

| Weak | Strong (Gargar-shaped) |
|------|-------------------------|
| “Support us” | “We need mentor feedback on metric honesty and one org + one junkshop window for 90 days” |
| “Funding to scale” | “Next: extend the pilot log to ≥100 kg and re-verify rates weekly — tell us which metric you’d trust on the wall” |
| “Partners welcome” | “If you know a Pasig MRF or campus org that will host a drop window, we want an intro this month” |

Close by **rewinding the mission line** (full circle): restate the 8–12 word offer, then the ask, then stop. Do not introduce new content after the ask.

### Time allocation (180 seconds)

Consensus pattern across 3-min startup coaching, 3MT, and showcase practice: **front-load problem + solution; compress team; never skip ask.**

| Block | Seconds | Words (~150 wpm) | Job |
|-------|---------|------------------|-----|
| **Hook + mission** | 0:00–0:20 | 40–50 | Attention + “what we are” in one line |
| **Problem** | 0:20–0:50 | 60–75 | Local pain + one stat + who + climate stake (short) |
| **Solution + why us / why this** | 0:50–1:15 | 50–60 | Gargar in plain language; benefit not features |
| **Demo / proof** | 1:15–2:15 | 80–100 *spoken* while UI moves | Show flow + 1–3 metrics |
| **Traction + limits** | 2:15–2:35 | 40–50 | Pilot numbers + honest non-claims / next test |
| **Ask + close** | 2:35–2:55 | 40–50 | Specific ask; full-circle mission |
| **Buffer** | 2:55–3:00 | — | Silence > overrun; judges hate cut-off midsentence |

**If cut to 2:30 on the day:** drop traction prose and team; keep hook → problem → demo → ask.

**Slide discipline:** few slides, sparse text. One idea per slide. Demo can replace 3 slides. Reading dense slides splits attention — audience does neither listening nor reading well.

### Common mistakes (student + climate pitches)

**Structure & content**

1. **Starting with the team** — judges don’t care yet; earn it with problem + proof.
2. **Pitching the technology / features first** — “we built an app that…” without friction.
3. **Problem = entire climate crisis** — unactionable; no clear buyer or user.
4. **Market = “everyone”** — classic student error; pick Ateneo orgs / Pasig households first.
5. **No user contact** — surveys-as-validation; prefer field talks with collectors / students.
6. **“We have no competitors”** — status quo *is* the competitor (sari-sari tips, Facebook groups, opacity).
7. **Traction theater** — press, likes, and logo salad without kg / users / retention.
8. **Carbon-credit distraction** — claiming credits, neutrality, or NDC-scale impact from a campus pilot.
9. **Hiding limits** — overclaiming rates, phone numbers, or flood causation; mentors punish this harder than small numbers.
10. **Fizzle ending** — demo ends, awkward “any questions?” with no ask.

**Delivery**

11. **Narrating the deck** (“On this slide you can see…”) instead of speaking to humans.
12. **Too much motion / text** — pacing, reading, and panel multitasking kill retention.
13. **Running over time** — in timed formats, overrun can mean disqualification or score cliff.
14. **Jargon wall** — MRF / EPR / NDC without one-line plain gloss (or skip the acronym).
15. **Impact-only framing** — passion without mechanism, unit of progress, or who pays/acts.

**Climate-specific credibility traps**

- Dramatizing existential risk instead of **mechanism + probability of your lever**.
- Confusing **potential** avoided emissions with **measured** diversion.
- Policy as base case (“when EPR fully kicks in…”) without a base path that works *now*.
- Treating informal workers as backdrop instead of **partners with price and dignity**.

### What “good” looks like for Gargar at showcase

| Signal judges reward | How Gargar shows it |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| Local problem fluency | Pasig / Metro Manila waste value opacity; basura → baha co-benefit careful language |
| Working loop | Live: rates → collector → diversion log |
| Honest metrics | ≥30 users, ≥3 collectors, ≥100 kg *or* proxy + plan (canvas SMART) |
| Justice beat | Transparency + fair path for informal recovery — not savior narrative |
| Mentor-safe climate | Methane diversion + drainage co-benefit; **explicit non-claims** if asked |
| Clear ask | Feedback on metrics + 1 org + 1 junkshop/MRF window |

## Gargar 3-min skeleton

**Spoken pace target:** ~145–155 wpm · **Hard stop:** 2:55 · **Claim freeze:** before Sep 5 showcase prep.

### 0:00–0:20 — Hook + mission (~45 words)

**Option A (contradiction):**  
“In Pasig, scrap already has a price — PET, cardboard, cans. But most of us never see that price, and we don’t know which collector will take it. So residual waste goes to landfill and waterways instead of recovery.”

**Mission line (≤12 words):**  
“**Gargar makes scrap value and a local collector visible in one flow.**”

**Option B (character):** open with one verified collector or student diversion moment, then mission line.

### 0:20–0:50 — Problem (~70 words)

- Households / students: no transparent rates → low sorting motivation.  
- Collectors / junkshops: uneven sorted supply.  
- Climate stake in one breath: more residual → landfill methane; plastics in drainage worsen local flood *aggravation* (adaptation co-benefit — do not claim flood control).  
- Justice stake: informal recovery already does the work; opacity keeps value and dignity off the table.  
- **One number** if solid (city residual share, pilot observation, or EcoWaste-backed rate example) — else skip rather than invent.

### 0:50–1:15 — Solution (~55 words)

“Gargar is a pilot tool: reference rates from field-backed sources, a simple ₱ calculator, a Pasig collector directory we’ve been verifying in person, and a diversion log so every handoff becomes proof — not a story.”

- Benefit language: *see the price → reach a collector → log the kg*.  
- Not: stack, scrape pipeline, roadmap features.

### 1:15–2:15 — Demo (~60–70s wall clock)

**On screen (single happy path):**

1. Open rate / calculator → show one material ₱ estimate.  
2. Open Pasig directory → one verified collector (name/area only; no dead phone claims).  
3. Open diversion log → add or show an entry; flash scoreboard: users / kg / collectors.

**Voiceover pattern:**  
“Watch a student price a bag of PET… find a collector in [barangay/area]… log the handoff. This is the loop we measure.”

**Backup:** Ivan’s 3-min (or 45s) recorded path if live fails.

### 2:15–2:35 — Traction + honest limits (~45 words)

- State pilot metrics as of freeze date (users, log entries, kg, collectors verified).  
- Limits in one line: “Rates are reference — we re-verify weekly; we are not a carbon product; we don’t claim national flood reform.”  
- Next test: hit or chase ≥100 kg + one ongoing campus/org window.

### 2:35–2:55 — Ask + full-circle close (~45 words)

“We’re asking this room for three things: candid feedback on whether our metrics are honest enough for a public wall; an introduction to one org and one junkshop or MRF that will keep a drop window open for ninety days; and partners who care about price transparency for informal recovery — not just another recycling selfie.

Gargar: see the value, reach a collector, prove the diversion. Help us keep that loop honest.”

**Stop. Smile. Hold for Q&A.**

### Rehearsal checklist (Story lane)

- [ ] Timed 3× with live demo; 1× with backup video only  
- [ ] Claim freeze list signed (rates, phones, kg, climate wording)  
- [ ] Justice beat coached (Charisse) — collectors as partners  
- [ ] One chart PNG max on proof slide (Alfonso)  
- [ ] Q&A prep: competitors = status quo; carbon credits = non-claim; scale = next 90 days not nation

### Cut list (if over time)

1. Drop team names entirely (put on canvas / exhibit).  
2. Drop second climate sentence (keep either methane *or* drainage, not both).  
3. Shorten demo to calculator + log only.  
4. Never cut the ask or the mission line.
