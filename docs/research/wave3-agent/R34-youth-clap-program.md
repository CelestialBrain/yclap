### 3. Legal & strategic rationale (from TOR)

1. **RA 9729 (as amended by RA 10174)** — CCC must enable multi-stakeholder participation and capacity for adaptation/mitigation; human-rights and climate-justice framing is explicit in the TOR background.
2. **Whole-of-government / whole-of-society** administration language — ACCELERATE is the CCC’s engagement mechanism across CSO, academe, private sector, LGUs, and development partners.
3. **Youth gap diagnosis (TOR):** Prior youth work was often **ad hoc** (e.g. CCC as SME for Climate Reality’s **Klima Eskwela**; university seminars). Partnerships with **NYC** and **DepEd** were described as *in incubation* at TOR time. YCLAP is positioned as a **more strategic, deliberate** youth intervention: knowledge-building → form/influence/mobilize young leaders → community benefit.
4. **UN SDG Moment 2021 “Welcome Generation”** — CCC’s defining youth rationale: a generation that refuses to be defined by pandemic + climate adversity; proactive, innovative, forward-moving.

### 4. Program design (TOR blueprint — high confidence for intended design)

**Model:** Pilot **accelerator** (design thinking + leadership + climate action), **not** a full semester course.

| Design element | TOR specification |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Learning spine** | **Five (5) modules** + comprehensive review |
| **F2F cadence (original 2025 plan)** | **Three full Saturdays** (in-person) + **asynchronous** materials within the delivery month |
| **Masterclass** | Half-day; **2–3** invited experts / youth champions / practitioners; openable to other CCC-invited youth |
| **MOB session** | “**Meeting of the Brains**” — facilitated brainstorming to prep the showcase |
| **Capstone** | One-day **Innovation Showcase** (exhibit + featured pavilion); tentative title **“Likha-Klima”**; recognition / reception |
| **Afterlife** | Launch **Young Climate Innovators Circle** (community of practice; social-channel based) |
| **Content themes** | Climate change science & policy; international agreements; youth movements; **leadership, advocacy & campaigning** principles/tools/case studies; climate **tools / innovative adaptation-mitigation projects** for communities |
| **Module packet** | Outline, learning objectives, lecture deck, case studies, references, glossary |
| **Governance of content** | **Design Reference Group (DRG)** — small ad hoc group (CCC + partners); inputs at inception + Delphi review of zero-draft modules |

**Dual competency the TOR emphasizes (what judges care about):**
1. Build climate tools **and/or** design advocacy/campaigning  
2. Youth **leadership skills to mobilize** climate action  

That maps cleanly to the 2026 student journey language: **Campaign Canvas + 3-minute pitch + pilot intervention** (internal program data).
