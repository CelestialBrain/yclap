## Title

EcoWaste Coalition and Mother Earth Foundation Philippines — campaigns, waste/toxics positions, and partnership angles for student waste–climate projects

**Context:** YCLAP research wave (Aug 2026). Flagship framing: Gargar scrap pilot + NGO intel — waste diversion as methane / landfill-load mitigation and informal-worker justice, **not** carbon credits, plastic neutrality, or WtE advocacy.

---

## Key findings

### EcoWaste Coalition (EWC)

1. **Who they are.** Multi-sector network founded **January 2000** (from the post–Clean Air Act Clean Air Coalition; founding conference convened with CBCP + Greenpeace). Vision: **zero waste + toxics-free** Philippines. ~**25 years** of service celebrated Feb 2025 (“Padayon: Sulong sa Hamon ng Panahon”). Leadership commonly cited: **Aileen Lucero** (National Coordinator), **Eileen Sison** (President). Member of **GAIA**, **Break Free From Plastic (BFFP)**, and the **IPEN** toxics network; partners include DENR-EMB, FDA, DOH, MMDA, and multiple LGUs (incl. **Pasig**, Quezon City, Manila, Davao, Baguio).

2. **Core campaigns (waste).**
   - **Defend RA 8749 incineration ban** and treat **waste-to-energy (WtE) incineration as a false solution** (toxic emissions, high cost, undermines source reduction; public pushback when executive/legislative moves threaten the ban, e.g. 2026 Marcos-era order debate).
   - **Plastic Free Pilipinas (PFP)** and annual **Plastic-Free July** (“Walang Plastikan, Walang Iwanan!”) — school + barangay clean-ups, **waste assessment** with **informal waste sector**, plastic-reduction ordinances; 2026 opening salvo: Manila High School + barangay + waste workers in Intramuros.
   - **Zero Waste Month** programming; ecological conduct of large events (SEA Games, World Meeting of Families) and faith festivals (Traslacion, Undas, Christmas).
   - **Anti–waste trade / foreign dumping:** forced re-export of **69 Canadian** + **364 South Korean** waste containers; JPEPA toxic-trade vigilance.
   - **E-waste:** non-combustion PCB destruction advocacy; UNIDO/DENR-linked CRT/PBDE collection (**~1,040 t CRTs**, **~90 t** PBDE plastics); youth e-waste concert (**~15,000** participants, **>8 t** collected).
   - **Informal-sector economic empowerment:** **BBEST** training for **504 women** in informal recycling; Women in Waste Economic Empowerment (WWEE) donor line.
   - **Schools / chemical-safe classrooms:** Brigada Eskwela work with **Ateneo School of Medicine and Public Health – ACRI** (lead-safe paint, toxic-free campuses; Pinyahan ES pilot); lead-safe paint certification track with paint industry + IPEN (Future Policy Award 2021 for lead-in-paint CCO).

3. **Core campaigns (toxics / consumer safety).** Heavy investigative product testing: **mercury** in skin-lightening creams (incl. online sales; 2024–2026 actions); **lead** in paints, school supplies, fast-food promo items; **phthalates/heavy metals** in toys; BPA in baby bottles (policy win); recycled **black plastics** with e-waste chemicals (Nov 2024); cleaner elections / reduced campaign litter.

4. **Positions that matter for YCLAP.**
   - **Hierarchy:** reduce volume + toxicity → segregate/reuse/recycle → residual only; **no incineration / no WtE-as-solution**.
   - **RA 9003 implementation first**, not new end-of-pipe tech.
   - **Producer / policy accountability** over pure consumer blame; plastics and toxics framed as **public health + environmental justice**.
   - **Just transition language** for waste workers and “leave no one behind” plastic-free framing (2026 PFP messaging).
   - **Climate link (implicit):** less residual → less landfill load / CH₄ / fire risk; less virgin material demand. EWC does **not** sell carbon products; climate is co-benefit of zero waste.

5. **Geographic / network fit for Ateneo–Pasig.** Explicit past partnership with **Ateneo ACRI**; honored **Pasig City** among LGU partners at 25th anniversary; MMDA and DENR-EMB relationships useful for narrative legitimacy, not for YCLAP to join national WtE fights.

### Mother Earth Foundation (MEF)

6. **Who they are.** NGO founded **1998** by ~15 eco-volunteers (often linked to Odette Alcantara’s circle and COCAP clean-air work). Chairperson **Sonia Sales Mendoza** (“Tita Sonia”) — former NSWMC NGO Commissioner (~2001), GAIA Asia-Pacific zero-waste woman of note. **Member of EcoWaste Coalition**, **GAIA**, **BFFP**, and climate/zero-waste networks (also cited with PNCC). Practical implementer of **RA 9003 at barangay/city scale**.

7. **Core model: Zero Waste Cities / communities.** Technical assistance + intensive IEC to LGUs: segregation at source, **no segregation–no collection**, MRFs, composting, plastic ordinances, **formalization of waste workers**. Flagship proof: **San Fernando City, Pampanga** (MEF partnership from ~2011; city-wide program under mayors Rodriguez/Santiago; plastic-free ordinance 2014 → bag ban; functional CSWMB with **waste-worker and youth seats**; ~**160 barangay-hired** workers with base pay + recyclables income). Other sites historically include **Malabon, Navotas, Batangas, Tacloban, Dumaguete, Siquijor** (first “Zero Waste Island” narrative), **Brgy. Fort Bonifacio (Taguig)**, Nueva Vizcaya, Puerto Princesa, school models (e.g. CRACMES, Holy Spirit ES Commonwealth QC).

8. **Programs students can copy or plug into.**
   - **JuanaZero** zero-waste / refill stores for low–mid income communities (Malabon launch; planned Batangas, Siquijor); **proceeds → Waste Workers’ Scholarship Fund** for children of workers at MEF sites.
   - **Zero Waste Youth Camp**, **Zero Waste Olympiad** (public + private schools); school inclusion in barangay ZW programs.
   - Training module: **Inner Ecology** (why) + **SWM hierarchy** (how: reduce/reuse/recycle + repair/repurpose/redesign).
   - Waste composition talking points used by MEF/GAIA (indicative household mix): ~**61% biodegradable**, ~**19% recyclable**, ~**16% residual**, ~**3% hazardous**; of residual plastic, large branded throwaway share (often-cited: top brands dominate — use for EPR/justice narrative, not invent new %).

9. **Positions that matter for YCLAP.**
   - **Zero waste is possible and better** than dumps/incinerators — community participation is the proof, not tech.
   - **False solutions** called out: **eco-bricks**, **cement co-processing of plastic as “fuel,”** WtE incineration, plastic credits / “neutrality” that leave production untouched (“turn off the faucet”).
   - **Waste workers as co-designers**, not afterthoughts (associations on SWM boards; scholarship fund; green jobs framing).
   - **Climate action framing** explicit in MEF/GAIA messaging: zero waste → conserve resources, cut fossil demand for virgin materials, cut landfill methane pathway.

### Relationship between the two

10. **MEF is a founding/core member of EcoWaste Coalition**; both sit in GAIA + BFFP PH. Division of labor in practice: **EWC = national campaign / toxics watchdog / media / multi-stakeholder pressure**; **MEF = on-ground LGU + barangay + school implementation**. BFFP PH plastics project historically lists **EWC + MEF + GAIA + Greenpeace SE Asia + HCWH** among collaborators. For student projects, treat them as **complementary allies**, not competitors.

## Ally angles

### For EcoWaste Coalition (primary “intel + legitimacy + school toxics” ally)

| Angle | Why it fits YCLAP / Gargar | Suggested ask (student-scale) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scrap diversion = landfill / methane co-benefit** | Aligns with EWC zero-waste + anti-landfill overload stance; does **not** require them to endorse carbon markets | 1 briefing with campaigner; quote + fact-check on residual vs recyclable streams for Pasig/Ateneo pitch |
| **Plastic Free Pilipinas / Plastic-Free July** | Already runs school + informal-worker joint waste assessments | Join or co-host **waste characterization** day on campus/barangay; publish kg diverted, not CO₂e |
| **Informal waste sector + BBEST / just transition** | Same justice lane as Gargar price transparency | Intro to women recyclers / junkshop network near Pasig; validate “fair rate” narrative |
| **Ateneo ACRI chemical-safe classrooms** | Existing EWC–Ateneo relationship | Brigada Eskwela-style **lead-safe / toxic-free** micro-project; cross-promote Gargar segregation |
| **Anti–false solutions discipline** | Keeps YCLAP off plastic credits, co-processing greenwash, WtE | Use EWC language when mentors ask about “offsets” or incineration |
| **Pasig LGU goodwill** | EWC already lists Pasig among partners | Soft intro via EWC if city ENRO/MRF access needed for pilot logistics |

**Do not ask EWC to:** co-brand carbon credits, plastic neutrality, or pro-WtE pilots. Stay upstream (reduce/divert/worker fairness).

### For Mother Earth Foundation (primary “how-to implement RA 9003” ally)

| Angle | Why it fits | Suggested ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barangay ZW playbook** | Proven IEC + segregation + MRF + no-segregation-no-collection | ½-day training for student cohort + partner barangay officers |
| **Waste-worker formalization model** (San Fernando) | Template for Gargar’s justice metrics (pay, association seat, recyclables income) | Case study visit or remote mentor session with MEF field staff |
| **JuanaZero / refill + scholarship** | Tangible low-income alternative to sachets; funds worker kids | Campus pop-up refill day; optional scholarship micro-donation from pilot proceeds |
| **Youth Camp / Olympiad** | Ready youth engagement formats | Enter Ateneo/Pasig school team; recruit volunteers for Gargar collection log |
| **False-solutions hygiene** | Eco-bricks / co-processing critique matches YCLAP non-claims | Cite MEF/Sonia Mendoza lines when challenged on “plastic fuel” or credits |

### Joint / network plays

- **One email / FB message pack:** (1) who YCLAP is, (2) 7-day Gargar pilot scope (kg diverted, rate transparency, collector list — **no credits**), (3) ask for 30-min call + waste-assessment methodology + Pasig/MM contacts.
- **BFFP / GAIA Asia Pacific** as secondary amplifiers if either NGO is bandwidth-constrained.
- **PNWWA / Magna Carta for waste workers** ecosystem (MEF + EWC adjacent) if justice framing deepens post-pilot.
- **Timeline fit:** Zero Waste Month (January) and Plastic-Free July = natural showcase windows; Brigada Eskwela for school toxics co-branding.

### Risks / boundary conditions

- Both orgs are **policy campaigners** — student pilots should **not** drag them into campus politics fights without clear mutual benefit.
- **Bandwidth:** national campaigns move fast; offer **labor** (volunteers, data collection, translation of findings into campus action), not only “endorsement.”
- Stay consistent with internal YCLAP non-claims: **no carbon product, no plastic neutrality, no WtE.** That is also their line — alignment is a feature.

**Medium–high (0.78)** on organizational identity, major campaigns, positions (anti-incineration/WtE, zero waste, toxics, waste-worker justice), and MEF–EWC complementary roles — grounded in org anniversary materials, GAIA/BFFP profiles, and multi-outlet 2019–2026 reporting.

**Medium (0.65)** on exact current staff titles, contact emails, and live project lists (websites partially stale; Facebook is often fresher). **Confirm contacts** via official FB pages / known campaigners before outreach.

**Medium (0.70)** on San Fernando and JuanaZero operational stats (older detailed case studies still widely cited; city figures may have shifted).

**High alignment confidence** that both orgs match YCLAP non-claims (no credits, no plastic neutrality, no WtE) and are suitable **allies for student waste–climate pilots** if students offer labor + transparent diversion metrics.

**Method note (Aug 2026):** Open-web synthesis only; no direct interview. Prefer primary press releases and GAIA/BFFP member pages over secondary paraphrases when pitching mentors.
