## Title

Ateneo SEEDS and ADMU sustainability / climate leadership programs — partners, track record (2020–2026), and Youth CLAP connection

**Context:** YCLAP research wave 3 (Aug 2026). Maps the institutional home of Ateneo’s Youth CLAP cohort (OAVP-SEEDS + Climate Change Commission), the parallel climate/sustainability stack (AIS, SUMP, ASOG, networks), and what public materials actually document.

**Naming note:** This brief is about **Philippine Youth CLAP** (Climate Change Commission **Youth Climate Leadership Accelerator Project** / related university cohorts). It is **not** the Odisha/India **YCLAP** (Youth4Water / Bakul Foundation “Young Climate Leaders Action Project”).

---

## Key findings

### 1. OAVP-SEEDS is Ateneo’s formal social–environmental engagement office — not a student org

- Full name: **Office of the Assistant Vice President for Social and Environmental Engagement for Development and Sustainability** (**OAVP-SEEDS** / **AVP-SEEDS**).
- Mandate (from title + public activity): integrate **social development**, **environmental engagement**, **service-learning**, formation, and multi-sector partnership under Mission Integration / university social responsibility — not a pure research institute and not a climate-only bureau.
- **Contact (public directory):** seeds@ateneo.edu · Social Development Complex, Loyola Heights · +63 2 8426 6001 loc 5033/5034.
- **Head:** **Dr Leland Joseph R. Dela Cruz** — Assistant Vice President for SEEDS; Associate Professor, Development Studies; long career arc through Development Studies chair → OSCI / social concern → student affairs / Associate Dean for Student Formation → AVP-SEEDS. Public scholarship and speaking emphasize **university social responsibility**, service-learning institutionalization, SDGs, and a **vulnerability lens** (vs pure poverty lens) for NGO–LGU–academe–business collaboration (AF General Assembly, 4 June 2025).
- **Staff publicly tied to SEEDS / escorts for YCLAP cohort materials:** Shenina Jolyn V. Badua (Assistant to the AVP-SEEDS; ASCEND / service-learning secretariat; GSDC 2026 Jakarta SUMP representative); Jack Lorenz Acebedo Rivera listed as escort on program materials (student-affairs professional; exact unit less visible on public org charts).

### 2. SEEDS Fund is the main hard public track record of OAVP-SEEDS (2024–2026)

- **SEEDS Fund** = small grants for **course-based service-learning, capstone, thesis/dissertation** with social impact.
- Caps (public): up to **₱5,000** per undergrad / ASHS project; up to **₱10,000** per graduate project (AGSB, ASOG, ALS, ASMPH, HE schools).
- **Documented disbursement (2nd semester SY 2024–2025):** **30 projects** (5 Senior High + 25 college); **₱285,301.24** total. Units included Biology, DISCS (CS/MIS — multi-semester campus mobility monitoring), Development Studies, Environmental Science (soil boron / agri locality), Health Sciences, Interdisciplinary Studies (Lokalpedia platform), Legal Management, Management.
- **SY 2025–2026:** fund reopened; application = letter to Dr Dela Cruz with faculty endorsement → seeds@ateneo.edu (guidelines linked from Ateneo news).
- **Service-learning prestige signal (2026):** Ateneo SL initiatives advanced as **finalists for Uniservitate Award 2026** (global Catholic higher-ed solidarity service-learning recognition) — institutional continuity for “formation + community impact,” not only one-off volunteerism.
- **Adjacent civic track:** OAVP-SEEDS + COA-M ran **COMELEC Register Anywhere** on campus (reported concluded **March 2026**) — shows SEEDS as a delivery partner for multi-stakeholder civic logistics, not only enviro grants.

**Implication of the track record:** SEEDS’ public footprint is **formation + subsidized community projects + civic partnership**, not large climate R&D budgets. Climate leadership for students is **hosted/escorted** here when programs are national (CCC), but technical climate depth lives more in **AIS / Manila Observatory / academic units**.

### 3. AIS is ADMU’s climate–sustainability technical hub (est. 2013); SEEDS is the engagement/formation AVP line

| Unit | Role in sustainability stack |
|------|------------------------------|
| **AIS (Ateneo Institute of Sustainability)** | Hub for environment–development agenda; Education / Research / Awareness / Services / Networking; Minor in Sustainability + MS Sustainability Management; campus + partner resilience work |
| **OAVP-SEEDS** | AVP-level social & environmental **engagement**, service-learning finance, multi-sector formation, student escorts for external programs |
| **Manila Observatory / AIS location** | AIS at Selga Hall, 2F Manila Observatory — physical co-location with long climate science tradition |
| **ASOG Environment & Climate Change Cluster** | Policy/training: climate & land use, CCA/DRR, negotiation/policy support, governance; CSO/academe seat on NDRRMC (external advisory listing) |
| **Student orgs / minors** | BOx, MEcO, AIS interns, Sustainability minor, Environmental Science Society, etc. — grassroots delivery layer SUMP explicitly wants to keep in implementation |

**AIS founding arc:** Established **2013** under Fr Jose Ramon T. Villarin as vehicle for environment–development agenda; now framed through **Laudato Si’ integral ecology** and Lux in Domino strategic goals: (1) mainstream sustainable development; (2) reduce disaster risk from climate/geohazards; (3) build a sustainable campus.

**AIS program pillars (public):**
- **Climate and Disaster Resilience (CDR)** — climate action in university policy; tools (systems thinking, literacy workshops); partner-community networks. Major products: Ateneo Climate Action Plan; Disaster Literacy Index Study; **My Climate Risk** WCRP regional hub; Systems Thinking for Resilience; **SECRA**; Integrated Urban Services Resilience Index (IUSRI); Sustainable Eco Theatre Packet; **Blue Map**; City Resilience Toolkit (CResT).
- **SDG / ESD Program** — Education for Sustainable Development modules; secretariat role for **AUN-EEC** (ASEAN University Network on Ecological Education and Culture); faculty ESD sessions (e.g. mid-2026).
- **Campus operationalization** — Biodiversity Action Plan; RE/solar partnerships (e.g. Shell Energy PH / SolX capacity narrative); Laudato Si’ Week / tree planting with student org partners; intern/volunteer calls (e.g. Aug 2026 intake).

**Leadership publicly visible 2025–2026:** Dr **Emmanuel D. Delocado** (AIS Director; biodiversity lab); Dr **Ma Assunta C. Cuyegkeng** (founding AIS director; Lily Gokongwei Ngochua Leadership Academy ED — continuity voice); CDR program head publicly named **Daniel C. Ratilla** (Climate and Disaster Resilience).

### 4. Flagship public material 2025–2026: Sustainable University Master Plan (SUMP)

- **Draft / consultation:** AIS-led; ~**27–28** community consultations; ~**18 months** process; *The GUIDON* coverage Sep 2025 called it the **first university sustainability master plan in the Philippines**.
- **Board of Trustees approval:** **15 November 2025**.
- **Public launch:** **12 February 2026** (mini-exhibit 9–13 Feb at Doghouse); Memo U2526-070 (Fr Roberto C. Yap, SJ).
- **Content structure (public summaries):** **7 principles** / seven-part definition of a sustainable Ateneo; **22 end states**; **55 targets** short (by **2027**), medium (**2030**), long (**2050**); triple bottom line (environment + social well-being + economic management); three-year review cycle; planned public **dashboard**; alignment with Climate Action Plan + Biodiversity Action Plan consultation tracks.
- **Long-horizon goals cited publicly:** carbon neutrality by 2050 pathway language; wastewater minimization; species diversity; green architecture / learning environments.
- **Speakers at launch explicitly bridged SEEDS + AIS:** Dr Dela Cruz (SEEDS / Laudato Si’ journey), Dr Delocado (SUMP process), Dr Cuyegkeng (historical sustainability journey). **SEEDS is not the author of SUMP; it is a co-legitimating AVP voice** for formation and community accountability.

**Access:** go.ateneo.edu/SustainableAteneo · PDF hosted on ateneo.edu (SUMP Digital Final, ~110 pp, AIS 2025).

### 6. Youth CLAP connection — CCC program, Ateneo via SEEDS

**What Youth CLAP is (PH):**
- Climate Change Commission–anchored **Youth Climate Leadership Accelerator Project** (procurement language: “Consulting Services for the Conduct of Youth Climate Leadership Accelerator Project,” ITB materials ~2025 on climate.gov.ph).
- Structure visible from cohort materials: **hybrid learning journey** (multiple Saturday sessions), **Campaign Canvas**, **Innovation Showcase** (~**12 Sep 2026** for current cohort), mentor/expert modules, multi-university youth cohorts.
- **CCC program contacts** (from Ateneo parents/program annex, public in internal YCLAP docs): **CJ Saga-Woo** (Office of the Vice Chair and Executive Director — cjwoo.ccc@gmail.com); **Ara Sace** (IKM / partnerships — partnershipsandcampaign@climate.gov.ph).

**What Ateneo’s role is:**
- **OAVP-SEEDS is the university institutional host/escort** for the Ateneo delegation (AVP Dela Cruz line; Badua / Rivera as escorts on session and showcase dates).
- Cohort size in current cycle materials: **~10 Ateneo student delegates** spanning ME, Applied Math–DS, MEc, CTM + Sustainability minor, Chem (AIS-linked), Physics, LEARN, OPCS, BOx leadership, etc.
- **Session experts** named in Learning Journey materials: **Dr Emma Porio** (urban vulnerability, gender, community-based climate risk — long CCC/youth lecture partner); **Dr Rodel Lasco** (OML Center; ecosystems / NbS / science-to-action); **Ms Vicky Tan** (project development / resource mobilization modules).
- Framing in Ateneo desk materials: **Youth CLAP = CCC + SEEDS** — national climate leadership program delivered with university formation/engagement office as on-campus anchor.

**What public web still lacks (as of Aug 2026 research pass):**
- No full public “Youth CLAP official landing page” with complete multi-university roster or fine-grained scoring rubric found in open search.
- No evidence that AIS *runs* Youth CLAP operations; AIS is the **natural campus continuity partner** after showcase (SUMP, internships, CDR tools, SDSN writing, Biodiversity/Climate Action Plans), while **SEEDS owns the student engagement relationship**.

## YCLAP implication

1. **Pitch to the right office for the right ask.**  
   - **SEEDS (Dela Cruz / Badua / Rivera):** formation quality, community benefit, vulnerability framing, multi-sector partners, service-learning continuity, escort logistics, showcase conduct.  
   - **AIS (Delocado / CDR / SDG teams):** science rigor, SUMP alignment, campus pilot continuity after 12 Sep, intern pathways, SDSN/AUN-EEC language, Climate Action Plan hooks.  
   - **CCC (Saga-Woo / Sace):** national program criteria, partnerships/comms, Innovation Showcase legitimacy.  
   Do **not** treat SEEDS as a climate lab or AIS as the YCLAP registrar.

2. **Match mentor and AVP values already on the public record.**  
   - Dela Cruz: **vulnerability + multi-sector collaboration + SDGs**, not tech theater.  
   - Porio: local urban risk, justice, evidence from place.  
   - Lasco: climate literacy + nature-based / science-to-action honesty (no one-day tree-planting cosplay).  
   - Tan: feasible canvas, partners, resources.  
   Gargar-as-flagship (scrap diversion + collector path + kg metric + methane/flood co-benefits) fits **SEEDS formation** *and* **AIS SUMP/SDG 12–13** better than carbon-credit or utility-RE LARPing.

3. **Use SUMP as institutional cover, not as the product.**  
   Name **campus circularity, waste, lifestyle, and measurable diversion** as student contribution to SUMP short-term (2027) culture-of-sustainability end states — then show **kg + users + partner junkshops**, not a 55-target rehash.

4. **SEEDS Fund is a post-showcase continuity option, not a YCLAP budget.**  
   If the pilot becomes a for-credit SL/capstone/thesis with a faculty adviser, **₱5k (or graduate ₱10k)** is real but small — design for **zero-cost channels + org mobilization** first; SEEDS Fund is a top-up, not a runway.

5. **Partner map for asks (realistic order):**  
   - Immediate: SEEDS escorts, cohort orgs (BOx, MEcO, AIS interns), EcoWaste evidence lane.  
   - Campus continuity: AIS SUMP / CDR / intern programs.  
   - National narrative: CCC showcase language (RA 9729 youth agency; waste as climate in CCC Ridge-to-Reef-type messaging).  
   - Stretch (post-demo): ASOG policy briefing, GIZ/Forest Foundation only if project geography matches their portfolios — do not name-drop networks without a live contact.

6. **Reputation guardrail.**  
   Public materials show ADMU investing in **integral ecology + SL quality + master-plan accountability**. Overclaiming (national methane inventories, plastic neutrality, Article 6 products) would clash with both SEEDS vulnerability ethics and AIS science culture. Stay **local, measurable, justice-aware**.
