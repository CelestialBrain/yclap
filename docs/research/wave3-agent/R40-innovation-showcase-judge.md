## Title

Innovation showcase / demo-day judging criteria for youth climate & social innovation — what panels score, prototype vs pitch, community-benefit evidence, and Sep 12 prep checklist

**Context:** YCLAP research wave (Aug 2026). Audience: mentors / CCC showcase + similar student climate demo days. Flagship application: Gargar pilot (rates + Pasig collectors + diversion log) with Campaign Canvas + 3-min pitch. Complements **R39** (pitch structure) and **R37** (honest SDG 12 / kg metrics).

---

## Key findings

### 1. What panels actually score (composite scorecard)

Youth climate and social-innovation showcases rarely publish identical rubrics, but **public scorecards converge**. Across ClimateLaunchpad (Climate-KIC), Hult Prize, MIT PKG IDEAS, UNDP/AIM Youth Co:Lab, Social Shifters Global Innovation Challenge, and Cleantech Open–style accelerators, judges score a stable core:

| Criterion cluster | What judges ask | Evidence they trust in ≤3–5 min |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Problem clarity** | Real pain? Named segment? Local enough to matter? | One place + one friction + one metric (not “climate change is bad”) |
| **Solution / innovation** | Practical intervention, not slogan; novel *in context* (tech **or** model **or** access design) | Working loop shown live or on video; “why this, not status quo” |
| **Impact (climate + social)** | SDG alignment; climate significance in *this* sector; who benefits | Measured pilot outputs now + honest theory of change for later |
| **Feasibility / execution** | Can this team run it in the next 30–90 days? Resource realism? | Field partners, pilot log, clear next test — not a 5-year megaproject |
| **Traction / validation** | Did anyone use it? Did anyone change behavior? | Users, kg, collectors, partner names, photos, dates |
| **Team / leadership** | Roles, completeness for the task, entrepreneurial grit | Lane map + who owns demo / science / mobilize / story |
| **Business or path sustainability** | How does it continue without the competition? | Afterlife (org window, LGU, SEEDS/AIS) — revenue optional at student stage |
| **Pitch quality** | Compelling under time pressure? | Clarity, pace, demo discipline, answerable Q&A |

**Hult Prize (universal criteria):** Team · Idea · Impact (SDG + real change by 2030) · Business. Progressive add-ons later: product–market fit / GTM (Nationals) → traction (Incubator) → scaling (Finals).

**ClimateLaunchpad (six criteria):** Business/market potential · Job potential or social impact · Innovation/technology potential · **Climate impact** · Strength of management team · **Quality of pitch**.

**MIT PKG IDEAS:** Innovation *in context* · Feasibility of implementation · Impact potential.

**Youth Co:Lab (UNDP/AIM pattern):** Problem statement · USP / theme alignment · Startup potential & scalability · Environmental/social impact · Business model viability.

**Social Shifters (youth social/env):** Impact · Feasibility · Innovation · Scalability — plus youth leadership and SDG alignment; eligibility often requires **already delivering some measurable outputs**.

**Cleantech Open–style:** Team · Technology (or model advantage) · Market size/growth · (plus environmental benefit and ability to execute in Nordic/peer variants).

**YCLAP-shaped translation (mentor / CCC showcase, not VC Demo Day):**

| Weight (practical) | Score for |
|--------------------|-----------|
| **Highest** | Local problem fluency + **working pilot loop** + **honest metrics** |
| **High** | Climate mechanism (methane / diversion / co-benefit) stated without greenwash; justice beat for informal recovery |
| **Medium** | Team completeness across lanes; next-90-days path |
| **Medium-low** | Scale fantasy, market TAM slides, polished brand film without field proof |
| **Penalty** | Carbon-credit / plastic-neutrality product claims; vapor features (“book a collector” without verified numbers); reading dense decks |

Judges under time pressure cannot diligence a company. They score **clarity, credibility, and care**. Impact is a **multiplier on a working loop**, not a substitute for one.

### 2. Prototype vs pitch — what wins on the day

| Mode | What it is | When it carries | When it fails |
|------|------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Pitch-only** | Narrative + slides + vision | Very early idea competitions that *select for coaching* | Showcase day after a multi-week program — looks like no field work |
| **Prototype-only** | App/demo without problem or ask | Hackathons scoring “execution” | Mentors ask “so what?” and “for whom?” |
| **Demo + proof (winner pattern)** | Live (or recorded) happy path **plus** 1–3 pilot numbers **plus** crisp ask | Student climate / social innovation showcases | Over-demo of edge cases; metrics without method |

**Consensus from demo-day and student innovation practice:**

1. **Show the thing.** In mentor/student climate formats, the demo is the truth serum. Judges trust what they see over what they hear.
2. **MVP that answers one hypothesis** beats a broad feature list. (~half of judge feedback in recent campus AI/demo research: focused prototype > do-everything UI.)
3. **Traction inoculates disbelief.** Even small numbers (30 users, 3 collectors, 100 kg) beat global tCO₂e theater.
4. **Learning is traction at early stage.** Interviews with junkshops/students, rate re-verification, rejection log for contaminated scrap — all count as validation.
5. **Backup demo is mandatory.** Wi‑Fi dies; have a 30–45s screen capture + offline board photo.
6. **Pitch quality is scored separately** (ClimateLaunchpad lists it explicitly). A great prototype with a muddled story loses to a clear story with a smaller but honest pilot.

**Prototype floor for Gargar (hard gates from Campaign Canvas):**

- Live path: **rate look-up → collector path → diversion log entry**
- Exportable log (users, kg, verification level, dates, place)
- ≥3 collectors verified **in person** before claiming them
- Claim freeze before pitch (see R37 claim ladder)
- **No showcase without pilot log** (canvas risk table)

**Pitch job (≤3 min):** hook → local problem → offer in plain language → demo + proof → limits/non-claims → specific ask. Full structure: **R39**.

**Rule of thumb for judges:** *Prototype proves you built; pilot proves someone used it; community evidence proves it matters to people beyond the team.*

### 3. Community-benefit evidence (what “impact” looks like without greenwash)

Social and climate panels punish unfalsifiable planetary claims. They reward **stakeholder-grounded, time-boxed, method-transparent** evidence.

#### 3.1 Evidence ladder (use the lowest true rung)

| Rung | Claim type | Acceptable evidence |
|------|------------|---------------------|
| **A — Engagement** | People tried the tool / attended window | User count, org lists, timestamps, screenshots |
| **B — Behavior / collection** | Material moved to recovery path | Weigh-ins, receipts, hand-off photos, collector confirmation |
| **C — Operational diversion** | Mass diverted from residual *path* (pilot definition) | B + material types accepted by shop |
| **D — Social / justice** | Fairer path / transparency for informal recovery | Named partners, fair-rate language, worker consent for stories |
| **E — Climate co-benefit** | Illustrative methane / landfill-load order-of-magnitude | Method card + uncertainty; **not** a credit |
| **F — Forbidden at student showcase** | Carbon offset, plastic neutrality, “saved the river,” SDG Indicator achieved | — |

Default safe line (from R37): pilot window + place + **N users** + **K kg** at hand-off to verified collectors — aligned with **SDG Target 12.5**, not Indicator 12.5.1, not a credit, not end-of-chain remanufacturing proof.

#### 3.2 What community-benefit panels look for

- **Defined beneficiaries** — students/households *and* informal collectors/junkshops (not savior narrative).
- **Proximity to the problem** — field visits, rate verification, barangay/campus node (Ashoka-style “connection and commitment” logic).
- **Stakeholder voice** — quote or photo with **consent**; justice claims need people, not app screenshots alone.
- **Do-no-harm** — no doxxing collectors; no inflated rates; no implying flood-control reform or EPR compliance product.
- **Replicability signal** — “another campus/org could run this window” beats “we will transform Metro Manila waste.”
- **Afterlife** — who owns the pilot after Sep 12 (AIS/SEEDS, EcoWaste feedback, org + MRF window).

#### 3.3 Metrics that score vs metrics that backfire

| Prefer on the wall | Avoid |
|--------------------|--------|
| Users who priced material; diversion log entries; kg **verified** vs estimated (split) | Blended kg without verification level |
| Collectors verified in person; material mix | “Market prices guaranteed” |
| Time-box: pilot dates on every number | Open-ended “we divert X% of Pasig” |
| Method card (1 slide): boundaries, formulas, exclusions | “Zero waste / carbon neutral / closed the loop” |
| Honest miss: rejection log, rate lag, phones not yet verified | Logo salad / likes as traction |

### 4. How a typical judging session works (so you prepare for the process)

1. **Pitch / demo** (live or recorded; often 2–4 minutes).
2. **Individual scoring** on a shared scorecard.
3. **Deliberation** — judges reconcile outliers; one strong skeptic can sink overclaims.
4. **Decision / ranking** — sometimes diversity of theme/stage is considered for the portfolio, but **the same scorecard still applies**.

**Q&A is where honesty is stress-tested.** Prepare 60-second answers for:

- How did you measure kg? Who weighed it?
- Why won’t this die after the showcase?
- Are you double-counting the same scrap?
- How is this climate (mechanism), not just recycling PR?
- What did informal workers say? Did you pay or only extract story?
- What’s your biggest unknown next month?

### 5. Failure modes judges punish hardest (student climate edition)

1. **Vaporware demo** — features claimed but not field-verified.
2. **Impact theater** — tCO₂e or “NDC contribution” from a campus pilot.
3. **Carbon / plastic credit product** as the hero (see deep-research brief: overhyped for youth innovators).
4. **Problem = entire climate crisis** — no buyer, no user, no lever.
5. **Market = everyone** — no first geography.
6. **Team intro first, proof never** — competence impression forms in ~30s; earn team later.
7. **Hiding limits** — mentors punish overclaim harder than small numbers.
8. **No ask** — “support us” is not a close; specific mentor/partner ask is.
9. **Running over time** — score cliff or hard stop mid-sentence.
10. **Erasing informal workers** — or tokenizing them without consent/fair path.

### 6. What “good” looks like for Gargar on showcase day

| Judge signal | Gargar proof |
|--------------|--------------|
| Local problem fluency | Pasig / Ateneo opacity of scrap rates; basura→baha as careful co-benefit (not sole flood cause) |
| Innovation in context | One flow: reference rates + directory + diversion log (access design, not new hardware) |
| Working prototype | Live happy path + offline video backup |
| Feasibility | Named collectors, org recruitment path, rate re-verify ritual |
| Impact honesty | ≥30 users, ≥3 collectors, ≥100 kg **or** honest proxy + plan; claim ladder A–C primary |
| Climate layer | Residual diversion → landfill methane / load (order-of-magnitude, method-card); non-claims ready |
| Justice layer | Transparency + fair path for informal recovery |
| Team | Four lanes visible if asked (Build / Science / Mobilize / Story) |
| Ask | Mentor feedback on metric honesty + 1 org + 1 junkshop/MRF window for 90 days |
| Afterlife | AIS/SEEDS continuity · EcoWaste feedback · optional EPR conversation (research only) |

## Sep 12 prep checklist

**Hard date:** Innovation Showcase / demo day — **12 September 2026**. Work backward from claim freeze and pilot close.

### T-minus ~5 weeks → T-minus 3 weeks (now → ~Aug 22)

- [ ] Lock flagship scope (default: **Gargar**); if pivot, re-scope Build immediately — lanes stay.
- [ ] Confirm **one geography** (Ateneo + one Pasig/QC node) and **one hero metric** (kg diverted + users).
- [ ] Science lane: climate–waste one-pager + methane method card draft (Clariz); justice talking points start (Charisse).
- [ ] Mobilize: org commitments path to **≥30 users** (Nathanielle/Sophia) — names, not aspirations.
- [ ] Build: stable demo path rates → collector → log; label rates **as-of date + source**.

### T-minus 3 weeks → T-minus 2 weeks (~Aug 22–29)

- [ ] **Field-verify** PET + Al cans at **≥2 Pasig junkshops**; fix lata/Al confusion in UI copy.
- [ ] In-person verify **≥3 collectors** before any “book / reach a collector” language.
- [ ] Prefer showcase trio: **Pineda MRF + 2 junkshops** (pilot plan) if logistics allow.
- [ ] Start diversion log with verification enum (`self_report` | `photo` | `weighed_*` | `receipt`).
- [ ] Consent protocol for photos/stories of collectors and students (showcase use).
- [ ] Campaign Canvas v1 frozen for mentor review (problem, SMART goal, metrics, non-claims).

### T-minus 2 weeks → T-minus 1 week (~Aug 29–Sep 5)

- [ ] **Claim freeze (Sep 5)** — Science owns; no new impact claims after freeze without log proof.
- [ ] Split **kg_verified** vs **kg_estimated** on scoreboard; never blend silently.
- [ ] Pitch script v1 (Mark) following R39 time boxes; justice beat coached (Charisse).
- [ ] Evidence kit: log export, kg board photo, hand-off photos, rate stamps, partner list (Ivan + Alfonso).
- [ ] Record **30–45s backup demo** video (offline-safe).
- [ ] Non-claims slide/mental checklist: no carbon credit, no plastic neutrality, no national flood reform, no “we recycled into bottles.”
- [ ] Q&A drill sheet (10 hard questions) — Science + Build answer bank.

### T-minus 7 days → T-minus 3 days (Sep 5–9)

- [ ] Full **3:00 run-through** timed; hard stop at **2:55**; drop team/traction prose first if cut to 2:30.
- [ ] Second rehearsal with **hostile mentor** role (poke metrics, justice, climate mechanism).
- [ ] Exhibit language: one-sentence mission (8–12 words) + three wall numbers + method footnote.
- [ ] AV checklist: laptop, dongle, hotspot, backup video, PDF deck, charger, offline rate snapshot.
- [ ] Confirm who speaks (primary + justice beat + demo driver); roles on a notecard.
- [ ] If kg &lt; 100: prepare **honest proxy + 90-day plan** language (canvas SMART allows this) — never invent kg.

### T-minus 2 days → day-of (Sep 10–12)

- [ ] Final rate re-verify (or clearly label last field date).
- [ ] Export final pilot log; print or offline-open scoreboard.
- [ ] Consent re-check for any human face on exhibit.
- [ ] Sleep, water, one full dress rehearsal morning-of if possible.
- [ ] **Day-of kit:** demo device offline-capable · backup video · canvas print · log PDF · Q&A card · ask card (mentor feedback + 1 org + 1 junkshop window).
- [ ] After pitch: capture judge feedback verbatim the same day; assign 90-day owners before the room dissolves.

### Day-of “scorecard self-grade” (30 seconds before you walk on)

1. Can a stranger state our problem after 20 seconds?  
2. Can they see a working loop in under a minute?  
3. Is every number on screen time-boxed and sourceable?  
4. Did we name who benefits besides us?  
5. Is our ask specific enough that a mentor can say yes/no today?

If any answer is no — cut scope of claims, not the demo.
