## Mentor tips

Use these when preparing modules 3–4 sessions, Campaign Canvas, and the Innovation Showcase ask. Assume **Tan rewards feasibility, partners, canvas, offtake path** (YCLAP internal mentor framing).

### Do

1. **Lead with a delivery system, not a slogan.** One beneficiary, one pain, one pilot path (e.g. Ateneo/Pasig students → transparent ₱ rates → named collectors → diversion log).
2. **Show a partner map like a conglomerate org chart.** Who operates? Who offtakes material? Who validates numbers? Who opens a door in 90 days? Label **confirmed vs hoped-for**.
3. **Make resource mobilization concrete.** Prefer: “one junkshop window + one campus org + mentor feedback on metric honesty for 90 days” over abstract “funding” or “scale nationwide.”
4. **Speak risk and co-benefit.** Frame waste diversion as **local risk reduction** (methane SLCP + drainage/flood co-benefit) and **fairer market access** — language adjacent to her risk + sustainability integration.
5. **Own the numbers and the non-claims.** Report pilot-scale kg/users honestly; explicitly **not** selling plastic/carbon credits or claiming national flood reform.
6. **Use SDG language carefully.** SDG 12.5 / 11.6 / 13 as alignment, with **actionable targets**, not wallpaper (mirrors her “SDGs as business model, not PR” line).
7. **Ask for enablement, not charity.** Partners and policy/enabling environment (access, data, intro) match how she talks about net-zero roadmaps needing government support.

### Don’t

1. **Vapor finance** — blended-finance decks, imaginary carbon revenue, or “impact investors next month” without a working pilot.
2. **CSR cosplay** — one-off tree planting or basura cleanup photos as the whole project (she has run **structured multi-year NbS**, not stunts).
3. **Tech flex without offtake** — app architecture without verified rates/collectors.
4. **Global doom as the pitch** — she has publicly stressed hope and concrete levers (energy, consumption, transport); local agency wins.
5. **Confuse her with ADB communications leadership** or assume she is a pure adaptation-finance academic like Lasco/Porio — she is a **corporate sustainability / risk / multi-sector mobilizer**.

### One-liner for Canvas / pitch (Tan-facing)

> *“We’re not raising a climate fund — we’re proving a **feasible circular loop** with named partners, honest kg, and a 90-day offtake path so diversion is investable later.”*
