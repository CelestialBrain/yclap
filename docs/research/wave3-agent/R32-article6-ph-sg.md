## Title

**PH–SG Article 6 is a sovereign infrastructure story: framework signed April 2026, methodology shortlist published, first ITMOs still years away — and student carbon “products” are the wrong scale**

---

## Key findings

### 1. What was signed (and what it is not)

| Item | Fact (as of mid-2026) |
|------|------------------------|
| **Instrument** | Philippines–Singapore **Implementation Agreement (IA)** under **Article 6.2** of the Paris Agreement |
| **Signed** | **30 April 2026**, ASEAN Climate Week, Manila |
| **Signatories** | SG: Minister Grace Fu (MSE / Trade Relations); PH: DENR Secretary **Juan Miguel T. Cuna** |
| **Precedent** | **First** bilateral Article 6 IA for the Philippines; builds on **August 2024 MoU** |
| **SG network** | As of **June 2026**, Singapore lists **11 IAs**: PNG, Ghana, Bhutan, Chile, Peru, Rwanda, Paraguay, Thailand, Vietnam, Mongolia, **Philippines** |
| **Legal function** | Binding bilateral framework for **generation, authorisation, corresponding adjustment, and international transfer** of high-integrity carbon credits (ITMOs) from PH mitigation activities |
| **Not yet** | Automatic credits; open public call for PH project applications (Ghana-style Stage A pipeline not yet mirrored in public PH materials); named **project-level pilot shortlist**; first PH→SG ITMO transfer |

Official SG factsheet (MTI, 30 Apr 2026): correspondingly adjusted credits under the IA may be used to (i) offset **up to 5%** of a company’s taxable emissions under Singapore’s **International Carbon Credits (ICC)** framework (from 1 Jan 2024), (ii) contribute to **NDCs** and other international mitigation purposes (e.g. **CORSIA**). Singapore commits **5% share of proceeds** from authorised credits under this IA to **climate adaptation in the Philippines**, and **2% OMGE cancellation** at first issuance (those units cannot be sold, traded, or counted toward any NDC).

**Joint Committee (governance):** Co-chairs — Singapore: **Director, Carbon Planning Division, National Environment Agency**; Philippines: **Undersecretary, DENR**. JC maintains the pre-approved methodology list and oversees IA administration. Detailed stage-by-stage authorisation process documents for PH were stated as **“to be published in due course”** at signing; by mid-2026 the IA text, **Annex A (methodologies)**, and **Annex C (JC ToR)** are on Singapore’s carbon-markets-cooperation site; full application forms / project register for PH are not yet at Ghana/Thailand maturity.

### 2. “Pilot shortlist” — what actually exists mid-2026

There is **no public, named list of authorised PH pilot projects** under the SG IA as of mid-2026. What exists is a **methodology and programme shortlist** (IA **Annex A**) plus **sector samples** DENR used in 2026 briefings — i.e. the **eligibility envelope**, not a project pipeline shortlist.

#### 2a. Annex A — pre-approved carbon crediting programmes (PH–SG IA)

| Programme | Role under IA |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Gold Standard for the Global Goals (GS4GG)** | Large meth set: soil organic carbon; rice methane (AWD-type); enteric / dairy methane; clean cookstoves & cooking devices; manure–biogas; landfill methane (decentralised organic waste + LFG flaring/use); black-carbon cookstoves; household efficient devices; 2–3 wheeled transport; shore-side electricity; suppressed-demand electrification / food / agri processing; saragassum/macroalgae; marine fuels; shipping EE; etc. |
| **Verified Carbon Standard (VCS / Verra)** | Energy efficiency & solid-waste diversion in sustainable communities; **campus clean energy & EE (VM0025)**; tidal wetland & seagrass restoration (VM0033); ARR (VM0047); REDD+ reduced deforestation (VM0048); landfill gas (Verra-ACM0001); recycling / e-waste / biomass waste revisions; EV charging; biochar; food loss & waste; fleet EE; improved agricultural land mgmt; etc. **CCB / SD VISta (or VCS-recognised SD standard) required** for SD co-benefits on VCS activities. |
| **Global Carbon Council (GCC)** | Grid/captive **renewable energy generation**; pumping EE; **animal manure & waste management energy**; desalination RE / water savings; landfill gas. |
| **Architecture for REDD+ Transactions (ART)** | **TREES v2.0** only. **HFLD approaches not eligible.** |

Joint Committee may update Annex A over time. Pre-approval ≠ automatic authorisation: Parties still assess applicability under domestic law at **Stage A** (project application to JC). Suspended / non-latest methodology versions can be refused.

#### 2b. DENR “sample eligible projects” (briefing framing, not authorised pilots)

In DENR Climate Change Service materials presented around the IA (Jan 2026 JCM matchmaking context; PH carbon-market deck):

- Energy efficiency and **waste diversion** activities in sustainable communities  
- **Tidal wetland and seagrass** restoration  
- **REDD+** under Environmental Excellence Standard (ART TREES lineage)  
- **Energy generation from animal manure and waste management**

These map cleanly onto Annex A entries (VCS VM0018 / VM0033 / ART TREES / GCC manure–waste / GS landfill & biogas). They are **illustrative eligibility**, not a competitive shortlist of PH sites with Letters of Authorisation.

#### 2c. Adjacent PH pipelines (not SG IA projects — but real supply-side context)

| Pipeline | Mid-2026 snapshot | Relevance to SG IA |
|----------|-------------------|--------------------|
| **CDM legacy** | ~**77** registered projects; ~**3 Mt** issued credits; **7** projects in transition toward Art. 6.4 | Institutional memory (DNA = DENR Secretary; TECs for energy/forestry/waste); transition is separate from SG 6.2 |
| **Japan–PH JCM** | 2017 MoC; **4** approved methodologies (incl. rice water mgmt, solar PV, run-of-river hydro, biomass power); **5** registered; **~30** pipeline; Nov 2025 JC adopted revised rules + REDD+/ARR guidelines; PIN for native-species ARR in Negros Occidental | Parallel cooperative approach; some technology types (solar, waste-to-energy, rice methane) **overlap** Annex A themes but **JCM ≠ SG ITMO** without separate authorisation |
| **DOE Circular DC2025-09-0018** | Energy-sector carbon-credit guidance (“unlocking clean energy through carbon credits”) | Sector readiness for RE / efficiency credits; not itself an Art. 6 authorisation |
| **Forest carbon** | Roadmap to readiness in voluntary forest carbon market **2026–2030** (for issuance); draft DAO on registration/monitoring of voluntary forest carbon projects (for issuance) | VCM / forest governance rails; Art. 6 authorisation still needs national framework + corresponding adjustment rules |
| **National carbon policy blueprint** | Inter-agency design **Apr–Dec 2025**; elements: integrity principles, eligible activities, authorisation process, fees/SOP, **national carbon registry**, institutional arrangements | Status (DENR): **ongoing consultations; for adoption via CCC resolution** — the missing domestic spine for large-scale ITMO host readiness |

**Bottom line on “pilot shortlist”:** Mid-2026 PH–SG reality = **methodology shortlist published + JC standing + domestic blueprint unfinished + no public SG-IA project register**. Anyone claiming a student or startup project is “on the Singapore pilot shortlist” is inventing a list that governments have not published.

### 3. ITMO timeline — framework year, not transfer year

#### 3a. Sequence that actually produces an ITMO

A correspondingly adjusted ITMO under this IA is not a voluntary offset badge. Minimum chain (mirrors Singapore’s more mature IAs, e.g. Ghana 4-stage process):

1. **Domestic host framework** — PH rules on what can leave the NDC (overselling risk), who authorises, how corresponding adjustments are applied and reported (BTR / Art. 6 reporting).  
2. **Mitigation activity design** — methodology from Annex A; baseline, additionality, MRV plan; SD safeguards.  
3. **Joint Committee Stage A** — note of intent / project application; undertakings (incl. SG participation rules).  
4. **Validation** by accredited VVB under the chosen programme (GS / VCS / GCC / ART).  
5. **Authorisation** of activity and of mitigation outcomes for international transfer (host + buyer side per IA).  
6. **Implementation + monitoring period** — first issuance only after a completed monitoring cycle and verification.  
7. **Corresponding adjustment** in PH accounting; transfer to SG Eligible Entity / offtake path.  
8. **SG-side use** — ICC tax offset (≤5%), NDC, CORSIA, or government offtake (SG requires a **binding offer of ≥30%** of authorised ITMOs to the Singapore Government under general IA participation rules).

#### 3b. Realistic calendar (mid-2026 assessment)

| Milestone | Best-case (aggressive) | Base-case (more likely) | Notes |
|-----------|------------------------|-------------------------|--------|
| IA signed | **Done (30 Apr 2026)** | — | Framework only |
| Full PH application package + public call | H2 2026 – H1 2027 | **2027** | “In due course” at signing; PH CCC blueprint not yet adopted |
| First projects enter JC Stage A | Late 2026 – 2027 | **2027–2028** | Developers will wait for clear PH authorisation fees, registry, CA policy |
| First activity authorisation | 2027 | **2028** | Depends on VVB capacity + JC cadence |
| First monitoring + verification complete | 2028 | **2028–2029** | Crediting period start ≠ issuance |
| **First PH→SG ITMO issuance / transfer** | Late 2028 | **2029+** | Global precedent: few completed Art. 6.2 transfers years after first MoUs (e.g. Switzerland–Thailand 2024; Switzerland–Ghana cookstove ITMOs July 2025) |
| Material volumes for SG tax / NDC | 2029–2030 | **Post-2030** | Landscape REDD+, utility RE, landfill gas — multi-year capex |

**Demand-side clock (Singapore):** Carbon tax **S$45/tCO₂e for 2026–2027** (from S$25 in 2024–25), path toward **S$50–80/t by 2030**; ICC offset still **capped at 5%** of taxable emissions — so SG demand is **high-integrity, limited-volume, compliance-adjacent**, not open-ended voluntary hype. Singapore already contracted **~2.175 Mt** nature-based credits (Sep 2025 RFP awards: Ghana, Peru, Paraguay) under other IAs — PH is **supply diversification**, not an instant offtake guarantee for every PH project.

**Supply-side clock (Philippines):** Without a finished **authorisation process + national registry + corresponding-adjustment policy**, the IA cannot move volumes. DENR’s own deck still has the carbon policy blueprint at **“ongoing consultations / CCC resolution.”** Parallel JCM (~30 pipeline) and CDM transition (7 projects) show institutional capacity building — not a ready ITMO factory.

**Honest headline:** Mid-2026 is **architecture year**. First PH–SG ITMOs are a **multi-year** outcome. Anyone selling “Singapore Article 6 credits from our 2026 campus pilot” is either confused or greenwashing.

### 4. Why student carbon products are the wrong scale

This is the YCLAP-critical finding. Article 6 under PH–SG is **state-to-state market infrastructure**. Student / campus “carbon credit products” fail on **scale, authority, capital, integrity, and timing**.

#### 4a. Scale mismatch (orders of magnitude)

| Dimension | What PH–SG / Singapore buyers actually need | What a student team can usually deliver |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Volume** | Tens of thousands to millions tCO₂e over multi-year crediting periods; SG offtake & tax market care about **bankable tonnes** | Typically **&lt;100–1,000 tCO₂e** lifestyle / campus reductions if rigorously counted — often much less |
| **Capex** | Landfill gas systems, grid RE, landscape REDD+, industrial EE — **millions USD**, multi-year construction | Demo budget, grant, or bootstrapped weeks-to-months |
| **Counterparty** | Host government authorisation; SG Eligible Entity (Singapore-incorporated, tax resident, ≥3 local CPF-contributing employees) or SG Government; VVBs; registries | Class project, org, or early startup with no CA authority |
| **Liability** | Corresponding adjustment hits **national GHG inventory / NDC**; overselling risk is a **sovereign** problem | No legal ability to authorise ITMOs or adjust the PH NDC |
| **Time** | 3–6+ years concept → first issuance (see §3) | Semester / 5-week accelerator / demo day |

Campus **emission reductions** can be real and valuable (scope 1–2 EE, waste diversion, rooftop solar *as operations*). Turning them into **tradable Article 6 ITMOs** or a **“carbon product” sold to Singapore** is a different animal — and almost never youth-appropriate.

*(Note: VCS **VM0025 Campus Clean Energy and Energy Efficiency** appears on Annex A. That methodology is designed for **institutional-scale** campus programmes under full VCS registration and host authorisation — not for a student club selling tokens. Presence on Annex A does not make student carbon startups viable.)*

#### 4b. Authority and integrity barriers students cannot clear

1. **Host-country authorisation** — Only the Philippines (via its future Article 6 / CCC–DENR machinery and the Joint Committee path) can authorise mitigation outcomes for international transfer. Students cannot self-authorise.  
2. **Corresponding adjustment** — Without CA, a credit is **not** an ITMO usable toward SG NDC/ICC under this IA. CA is a **national accounting act**.  
3. **Additionality & baseline** — Youth “we planted trees / sorted scrap / turned off lights” rarely meets programme additionality tests against regulatory baselines (e.g. RA 9003, grid expansion, EPR).  
4. **Permanence & leakage** (especially nature-based) — Mangrove / forest / seagrass claims require tenure, long monitoring, and buffers; PH blue-carbon science already shows mass-planting failure modes (see R28).  
5. **Double counting** — Same tonne cannot count for PH NDC *and* SG tax/NDC; students have no registry position to prevent this.  
6. **SG participation rules** — Qualifying sale/transfer to an **Eligible Entity**, 30% government offer, OMGE/SOP — corporate/sovereign plumbing, not campus storefront.  
7. **Cost of integrity** — Validation, verification, registry fees, legal counsel, and JC process costs routinely exceed **any revenue** a micro student project could earn at honest prices.

#### 4c. Wrong product incentives for youth climate work

- **Demo-day pressure** rewards “we sell carbon credits to Singapore” pitch theater over measurable local service (MRF throughput, heat-risk interventions, flood last-mile, just transition for waste workers).  
- **Offset culture** teaches buyers that residual emissions can be cancelled cheaply — the opposite of the integrity bar Article 6 is trying (imperfectly) to raise.  
- **Tenure and land politics** (forest, mangrove, waste sites) are sites of elite capture risk; youth-led credit schemes without PO/CBFMA/LGU grounding repeat known failures.  
- **Parallel low-integrity markets** (weak voluntary offsets, plastic “neutrality” credits — see R04) already confuse the public; student products add noise, not ambition.

#### 4d. What *is* the right scale for YCLAP (study, not product)

| Do (research / program) | Don’t (product) |
|-------------------------|-----------------|
| Map Annex A methodologies to PH **waste, energy, FOLU** NDC PAMs | Sell “Article 6-ready” credits from a campus pilot |
| Explain corresponding adjustment, OMGE 2%, SOP 5%, ICC 5% cap in plain language | Promise Singapore offtake or NDC use |
| Track CCC blueprint / national registry / first JC calls as **policy milestones** | Claim a team project is on a government pilot shortlist without a public LoA |
| Partner with LGUs/POs on **real abatement** (methane capture prep, diversion data, EE audits) without claiming ITMO status | Tokenise tonnes or mint micro-credits for demo day |
| Use PH–SG IA as a **case study in climate finance architecture** | Build a startup whose unit economics require ITMO prices and sovereign CA |

**One-line rule:** Article 6 ITMOs are **sovereign-scale climate accounting instruments**. YCLAP cohorts should **understand them**. They should not **ship them**.

## YCLAP implication (study not product)

1. **Treat PH–SG Article 6 as curriculum and policy literacy**, not a venture thesis. Mid-2026 is the year the **rails** appeared (IA + Annex A + JC), not the year **trains** (ITMOs) run.

2. **Do not approve cohort projects whose primary deliverable is a carbon credit, ITMO, offset token, or “Singapore-ready” tonne.** Reject demo pitches that skip host authorisation, corresponding adjustment, and multi-year MRV.

3. **If waste / methane / EE / nature themes attract teams**, force **operational impact metrics** (tonnes diverted, CH₄ avoided under LGU monitoring, kWh saved, partner-validated) **without** credit issuance claims. Align to RA 9003, EPR, LGU solid-waste, or PO tenure — not to Annex A cosplay.

4. **Use Annex A as a research object:** Which methodologies map to Metro Manila waste and flood co-benefits? Which require landscape tenure YCLAP cannot secure? That analysis is high-value; selling the resulting “product” is not.

5. **Watch the real milestones** for future curriculum updates: CCC resolution on carbon framework; national registry live; first public PH–SG call for applications; first JC authorisation; first CA reported in a PH BTR. Until several of those land, “ITMO startup” remains science fiction.

6. **Scale honesty is climate integrity.** Student agency is real on **local systems change, data, and justice**. It is not real on **sovereign carbon markets**. Confusing the two produces greenwash and wasted talent.
