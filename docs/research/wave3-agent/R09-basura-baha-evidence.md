# R09 — Basura → Baha: Solid Waste Clogging Drains & Urban Flooding in Metro Manila / PH

**Agent:** wave3 research  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** Evidence linking solid waste (plastic/garbage) clogging drains, esteros, and pumping stations to urban flooding in Metro Manila and the Philippines — official statements, typhoon postmortems, project design, and quantified blockage figures where available.

---

## Key findings

### 1. Causal link is official and repeated — not fringe narrative

Philippine flood-management authorities consistently treat mismanaged solid waste as a **primary co-driver** of Metro Manila flooding (alongside outdated drainage capacity, extreme rainfall, siltation, and informal settlement in waterways):

| Actor | Claim | When |
|-------|--------|------|
| **MMDA Chair Benhur Abalos** | Garbage is the **"number one cause"** of flooding in Metro Manila because it blocks drainage; ~273 waterways must stay clear | Jun 2021 (rainy-season start appeal) |
| **MMDA GM Procopio Lipana** | EDSA flood (Sep 2023) caused by **garbage clogging highway drains** — plywood panel + plastic materials impeded flow | Sep 2023 |
| **President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.** | Post-Carina ocular: garbage **blocks pumps** so they are "not as effective as [they] could be"; urged proper disposal | Jul 2024 |
| **DPWH Sec. Manuel Bonoan** | **~70% of NCR internal drainage** is "silted sa mga basura and other things"; system only **~30% efficient** | Jul 30, 2024 (post-Carina Palace briefing) |
| **MMDA (post-Carina statement)** | Garbage thrown anywhere clogged street drainage; rainwater could not flow; waterways overflowed | Jul–Aug 2024 |
| **MMDA Chair Don Artes** | Drainage is >50 years old, narrow; **clogged with garbage** (items as large as sofa/refrigerator) makes floods hard to drain | Jul 2025 |
| **MMDA GM Nicolas Torre III** | **Garbage remains the major / top cause** of Metro flooding despite months of declogging; ~**7,000 t/yr waterways + 5,000 t/yr pumping stations** (~12,000 t/yr); **30–35 t/day** hauled from waterways & pumps | May 2026 / Jul 2026 |

**Mechanism (official framing):** Litter → streets/esteros → canal inlets & trash racks → **reduced conveyance + pump inefficiency** (plastic wraps motors, bulky debris jams rakes) → flood rises faster, subsides slower.

**Caveat for pitch honesty:** Officials almost always pair basura with **antiquated/undersized drainage** and **extreme rain intensity**. Basura is a major **amplifying** cause, not the sole cause. Carina rainfall (~74 mm/hr cited by MMDA) far exceeded stated system design capacity (~24–25 mm/hr).

### 2. Quantified blockage / waste load figures (best available)

| Metric | Figure | Source / caveat |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Internal drainage efficiency (NCR)** | **~30% efficient** because **~70% silted with garbage + other material** | DPWH Sec. Bonoan, Jul 2024 (cites "a study done before"); **garbage + silt combined**, not plastic-only |
| **Post-Carina trash haul (NCR)** | **1,513 tons** collected Jul 24–Aug 5, 2024 from rivers, esteros, canals | MMDA / GMA; MMDA said this trash **blocked drainage and contributed to flooding** |
| **Annual haul (MMDA)** | **~12,000 tons/yr** (~7,000 waterways + ~5,000 pumping stations) | Torre, May 2026 (Philstar) |
| **Daily haul (waterways + pumps)** | **30–35 tons/day** (~1,000 t/month) | Torre, Jul 2026 (Tribune) |
| **Pumping-station trash (short window)** | **526.8 tons** from 71 PSs in ~5 days (Jul 18–22, 2025) plus street collections | PNA / MMDA reporting |
| **World Bank MMFMP Component 2 target** | Cut solid waste collected at selected pumping stations **by half** (~**8,500 m³** reduction target); Component 2 budget ~**US$48M** (IBRD+AIIB+GoP) | World Bank MMFMP restructuring paper (2024) |
| **Upstream barangay SWM** | Survey during MMFMP found waste at PSs is **primarily from mismanaged waste in upstream barangays** → project pivoted to community-based SWM (231 barangays engaged by Sep 2024) | World Bank MMFMP restructuring paper |
| **PH plastic generation (context)** | ~**2.7 Mt plastic waste/year**; ~**20%** ends in ocean; **~163–164M sachets/day** + tens of millions of bags (GAIA / World Bank plastics study cited by CCC) | Climate Change Commission (Sep 2024); Oceana PH |
| **Plastic share of drain solids (global/LMIC analog, not MM-specific)** | Common cite: solids in drains ~**80% plastic**, occupying ~**20% of drain volume** (Isale Koko-type studies; Tearfund plastic-flooding report) | **Not a Metro Manila field measurement** — use as comparative mechanism evidence only |
| **Hydraulic lab evidence (SE Asia, relevant to Manila)** | Plastic debris forms **denser, faster blockages** than organic matter (fewer voids); flume + field (Bandung) estimated **~1 m backwater rise in ~1 hour** at trash rack under rainy-season conditions; authors explicitly list **Manila** as a relevant city | Honingh et al., *Frontiers in Earth Science* (2020) |

**What is *not* cleanly quantified for Metro Manila:**

- No widely published, peer-reviewed **plastic-only % of Metro Manila drainage blockage volume** (e.g. “X% of clog mass is sachets”).
- The **70%** figure is **garbage + silt + other**, restated by DPWH secretary from a prior study — secondary sources (Oceana) sometimes compress this to “clogged with garbage and silt, much of it plastic.”
- Capacity loss is multi-factor: clogging × undersized pipes × land subsidence/impervious cover × extreme rain.

### 3. Typhoon / extreme-rain postmortems

#### Typhoon Carina (Gaemi) + enhanced Habagat — Jul 2024

- Severe Metro Manila inundation; NCR under state of calamity; ~125k persons affected in NCR alone (NDRRMC-era tallies in press).
- **DPWH Bonoan:** 70% internal drainage silted with basura/other → flooding in some areas.
- **President Marcos:** Improper waste disposal blocked pumps; high tide + heavy rain compounded effect.
- **MMDA:** 1,513 tons of trash hauled post-event from waterways/canals; stated basura clogged street drains and caused overflow; also stressed system capacity gap (design ~25 mm/hr vs observed ~74 mm/hr).
- **Climate Change Commission** (Sep 2024 “Ridge to Reef”): Carina and TS Enteng as examples where **improperly disposed plastics, food wrappers, and debris** blocked waterways, preventing floodwaters from receding; highlighted Talayan Creek (Quezon City) as visible waste-clog site after Enteng.

#### EDSA flood — Sep 2023 (localized postmortem)

- MMDA attributed heavy flooding on EDSA northbound (Ortigas / Camp Aguinaldo area) to **garbage clogging drains** (plywood + plastics), despite continuous cleaning — classic “cleanup is not enough if inflow continues” evidence.

#### Broader pattern (Ondoy frame)

- Officials and press routinely compare later floods to **Ondoy (2009)**; Carina narrative was “less rain than Ondoy in some comparisons, worse effects in others” — used to argue that **infrastructure + waste + urbanization** degraded resilience, not only rainfall volume.

### 4. Structural recognition: flood control *includes* waste management

The World Bank / AIIB **Metro Manila Flood Management Project (MMFMP, P153814)** is design-level proof that international and national flood policy treats solid waste as flood infrastructure:

- **PDO:** Improve flood management in selected Metro Manila areas.
- **Component 2 — Minimizing Solid Waste in Waterways** (~US$48M): improve SWM near drainage systems served by pumping stations; **halve solid waste volume at PSs** (~8,500 m³ target).
- Rationale restated in 2024 restructuring: waste at pumps comes **primarily from mismanaged upstream barangay waste**.
- Implementation includes community-based SWM (hundreds of barangays), trash traps, MRFs, waste conversion pilots at PSs (Vitas granulator/brick-making, etc.), and a Metro solid-waste master-plan track.
- Component 1 modernizes/rehabilitates pumping stations — still undermined if trash racks and intakes choke.

**Implication for YCLAP:** You do not need to invent the basura–baha theory. **DPWH, MMDA, Malacañang, CCC, and the World Bank already built it into policy and budgets.** The gap is **last-mile diversion, behavior, collector livelihoods, and measurable kg kept out of esteros** — not proof of concept.

### 5. Academic / technical mechanism evidence

| Source | Finding | Relevance to PH |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Honingh et al. 2020** (Frontiers; Delft TU + ITB Bandung) | Plastic clogs trash racks **faster and denser** than organic debris; can drive rapid upstream water-level rise; lists **Manila** among cities where findings apply | Strong mechanism paper for “why plastic matters more than leaves” |
| **Tearfund 2023** plastic pollution & flooding report | Synthesizes LMIC evidence that plastic pollution reduces drain serviceability; cites drain-composition studies (~80% plastic of solid waste in drains in some sites) | Useful secondary; **not PH-primary field data** |
| **PH student / local engineering work** | Multiple theses and local papers model waste accumulation reducing canal capacity (e.g. Armitage & Rooseboom waste-load formulas; DPWH Region 3 40–60% clog ranges cited in student work) | Supports engineering framing; quality varies |
| **Oceana Philippines 2025** “Choking our cities…” | Public advocacy piece tying sachets/bags to drain clogging; cites DPWH 70% figure and flood-control spend still failing under clogged waterways | Good narrative source; verify numbers against primary DPWH quotes |

### 6. Composition of the clog (what actually blocks)

From MMDA field reports and inspections (not a full composition audit):

- **Plastics:** bags, bottles, wrappers, sachets — entangle pump motors, form dense mats at racks.
- **Bulky waste:** plywood, furniture, sofas, refrigerators, tires, tree trunks (Tripa de Gallina / other PS inspections).
- **Silt / grease / organics:** co-drivers of the 70% “silted with basura and other things” figure.
- **Water hyacinth:** often co-removed; MMDA has explored hyacinth-to-coal processing.

Plastic is the **politically and pedagogically salient** fraction (ubiquitous, non-biodegradable, flexible → dense gates), but honest messaging says **basura + silt + undersized drains + extreme rain**.

## YCLAP pitch framing

### Core claim (defensible)

> **In Metro Manila, mismanaged solid waste does not just dirty the bay — it sabotages flood infrastructure.** Officials from MMDA, DPWH, and Malacañang, plus World Bank flood-project design, treat garbage clogging drains and pumps as a **primary reason floods rise faster and recede slower**. Diversion of scrap and organics from streets and esteros is therefore **climate adaptation infrastructure**, not only “cleanliness” or “recycling.”

### One-liner options

1. **Basura = baha infrastructure failure.** Keeping kg out of drains is flood control without pouring concrete.
2. **Pumps can’t beat plastic.** MMDA hauls tens of tons a day from waterways and pumps; Carina left 1,500+ tons in the network.
3. **Flood budgets already fund waste** (MMFMP Component 2). Community diversion and collector systems are the missing last mile.

### How to use numbers (safe hierarchy)

| Use freely | Use with caveat | Avoid |
|------------|-----------------|-------|
| 70% internal drainage silted with basura + other (DPWH 2024); ~30% efficient | “Mostly plastic” — only if you say “much of urban litter is plastic/sachets,” not that 70% is plastic | Claiming plastic alone causes 70% of Metro floods |
| 1,513 t post-Carina; ~12,000 t/yr MMDA waterway+PS haul; 30–35 t/day | 80% plastic / 20% volume (foreign drain studies) as **analog**, not MM measurement | “Garbage is the only cause” (officials always add capacity + rain) |
| MMFMP Component 2 exists to cut PS waste by half (~8,500 m³) | Abalos “number one cause” as **political diagnosis**, not a hydrologic attribution study | Unsourced “studies show X% of floods are plastic” |

### Flagship fit (Gargar / scrap-to-value / SWM)

- **Mitigation co-benefit:** less landfill methane + less plastic leakage.
- **Adaptation co-benefit:** less mass available to clog inlets, esteros, trash racks, and pump intakes → **flood resilience**.
- **Justice co-benefit:** informal collectors are the real upstream interception system that MMFMP-style barangay SWM needs.
- **Do not** overclaim: a campus/barangay diversion pilot will not “solve Metro floods”; it **demonstrates measurable kg diverted from the flood pathway** and models the last-mile piece government projects still struggle to deliver.

### Framing against flood-control corruption discourse (2024–2026)

Public anger after big floods often targets “ghost” or ineffective flood-control projects. Honest YCLAP line:

> Even perfect concrete fails if intakes are full of sachets and sofas. Waste diversion is the **low-tech half of flood control** that students and barangays can ship.

## Confidence

| Claim tier | Confidence | Notes |
|------------|------------|-------|
| Solid waste **contributes materially** to Metro Manila flooding via clogged drains/pumps/esteros | **High** | Multi-year, multi-agency consensus (MMDA, DPWH, President, CCC, World Bank project design) |
| **70% internal drainage** compromised by garbage + silt (~30% efficient) | **Medium–high** as official 2024 DPWH statement; **medium** as to underlying study methodology (public quote, full study not re-read here) |
| Post-event **tonnage hauls** (1,513 t Carina window; ~12k t/yr; 30–35 t/day) | **High** as MMDA operational claims reported in major outlets |
| Plastic specifically **worse than organic** at forming dense hydraulic blockages | **High** (lab + SE Asia field; Honingh 2020); **medium** for direct Manila flume calibration |
| Exact **% of MM drainage blockage volume that is plastic** | **Low / not established** in public peer-reviewed MM field audit — do not invent |
| Waste diversion **alone** eliminates Metro floods | **False / overclaim** — capacity, climate extremes, silt, and land use remain dominant co-drivers |
| YCLAP-scale diversion is **legible as flood co-benefit** | **High for framing**; impact is **local/demo-scale**, not metro-scale hydrology |

**Overall confidence for YCLAP use of basura→baha as a core adaptation narrative: High**, provided numbers are attributed, multi-causality is acknowledged, and plastic-only percentages are not fabricated.
