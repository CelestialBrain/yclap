### E. Structural market facts (2020–2026)

1. **Informal junkshops already are the scrap marketplace.** Academic and field work (e.g. city junkshop recovery studies; Context/Licas 2025 on TEZWA) show formal apps rarely replace them — they either **digitize**, **bypass**, or **ignore** them.
2. **EPR Act (RA 11898, 2022)** shifted capital toward **brand compliance and PROs** (targets stepping 20% → 80% recovery/offset). That money rarely funds transparent household rate tools.
3. **Reward apps** (points, leaderboards, AI scan) repeatedly rediscover the same funnel: awareness → deposit → redeem. They **fail** when: drop-off density is low, rewards are thin, offtake is intermittent, or contamination kills buyer trust.
4. **No dominant national “Grab for junkshops”** emerged 2020–2026 for multi-material household scrap with live pesos-per-kg. Closest mass players are **Plastic Bank** (collector network + brands) and **Rezbin** (B2B smart bins), not open scrap price boards.
5. **Plastic-credit / “neutrality” products** are reputationally radioactive for youth climate pitches post-PCX investigations — even if some credits fund real collection.

## What worked vs failed (patterns)

### Worked

| Pattern | Evidence examples |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Attach to offtake money** (brand EPR, corporate ESG, LGU contract) | Plastic Bank PRO sales; Rezbin office programs; QC Trash to Cashback |
| **Physical nodes + simple digital ledger** | Precious Plastic app; TEZWA junkshop; booth collection (TrashCash drives) |
| **Narrow material + clear buyer** | PET/HDPE buy-back; e-waste with data destruction (Edispo pivot) |
| **Justice framing with real workers** | Plastic Bank member stories; community associations; EcoWaste IWS advocacy |
| **Pivot from pure C2C marketplace to services** | Edispo: listings → B2B matchmaking + hazardous compliance |

### Failed or stalled (or chronically thin)

| Pattern | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **App-only scrap marketplace with thin supply/demand** | Two-sided liquidity; households have 2–5 kg; junkshops already have walk-ins |
| **On-demand multi-material pickup as pure startup** | Fuel + labor > scrap margin without subsidy (Trash Panda–class economics) |
| **AI recognition as moat** | Sorting still needs human/buyer acceptance; AI is nice-to-have education, not offtake |
| **Points without durable partner rewards** | Users churn when redemption is slow or low-value |
| **Plastic credits as youth flagship** | Integrity controversies (PCX co-processing share); CCC/mentor-safe climate story is **methane diversion + flood co-benefit + worker fairness**, not neutrality certificates |
| **National scale before verified local collectors** | Directory with dead phones = trust collapse (Gargar’s own known risk) |

## Differentiation map vs Gargar

| Dimension | Typical competitor | **Gargar wedge** |
|-----------|--------------------|------------------|
| Job to be done | Earn points / brand offset / book a truck | **See reference ₱/kg + reach a verified Pasig collector in one flow** |
| Primary user | Brand, office employee, registered collector | **Student + nearby household** (Ateneo / Pasig pilot) |
| Price signal | Opaque or points-only | **EcoWaste-field reference rates** (labeled as reference, re-verified) |
| Climate claim | Plastic neutrality / Social Plastic / ESG dashboard | **Landfill load + methane framing + basura→baha** — **no carbon/plastic credits** |
| Justice claim | Often afterthought or marketing photo | **Informal recovery path + price transparency** as justice |
| Proof | Corporate tonnage certificates | **Diversion log ≥100 kg, ≥30 users, ≥3 field-verified collectors** |
| Scope | National PRO ambition | **Hyperlocal pilot that can finish in 5 weeks** |

**Non-competitors (do not waste pitch time fighting them):** PCX credit buyers, Green Antz material science, Resiklo machine sales, Coca-Cola mall drop campaigns — different layers of the stack.

**Watchlist (same UX gravity well):** **Rezbin**, **Circulo+/TrashCash**, **Trash Panda**, any new “smart recycle” mall app — copy their deposit UX lessons; do **not** copy their B2B/bin CapEx or brand-PRO positioning for the showcase.

## YCLAP–Gargar competitive implication

1. **Do not pitch Gargar as “the recycling app for the Philippines.”** That category is crowded and capital-intensive. Pitch: **Pasig/Ateneo scrap-to-value pilot** — rates + collector path + honest kg log.
2. **Occupy the empty cell:** multi-material **price transparency + informal collector directory** with **field verification**. Plastic Bank owns collectors-for-brands; Rezbin owns offices; nobody owns a trustworthy student-facing **₱ reference board + MRF/junkshop handoff** tied to EcoWaste intel.
3. **Hard product rules from competitor autopsies:**
   - Never claim live booking until phones verified (TrashCash/Rezbin succeed only where a physical node exists).
   - Label rates **reference**; re-verify weekly (junkshop prices move).
   - Diversion log > vanity installs (TrashCash 10K downloads ≠ system change).
4. **Climate + justice narrative advantage:** Competitors either sell **compliance** (EPR) or **rewards**. Gargar can sell **mitigation co-benefit (methane/residual) + flood co-benefit + worker dignity** without PCX-style offset claims — aligned with YCLAP research brief overhyped list.
5. **Partnership, not rivalry:** EcoWaste Coalition (intel), 1–2 Pasig junkshops/MRF (Pineda-type nodes), optional later chat with Plastic Bank/Rezbin only if afterlife needs offtake — not for week-1 demo.
6. **Afterlife options (post-showcase):** (a) LGU Trash-to-Cashback-style points on top of rates; (b) feed verified kg to an EPR partner as **honest diversion data** (not a credit product); (c) open-source rate board for other campuses. Avoid building PRO infrastructure in 5 weeks.
7. **Youth angle:** Campus campaigns (booths, org challenges) work when paired with a **fixed weekly handoff window** — TEZWA/booth models beat pure on-demand pickup for pilot honesty.
