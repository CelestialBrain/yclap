## Key findings

### 1. “CELA” is not a documented Ateneo green/climate student org

- Exhaustive public search (Ateneo.edu, The GUIDON, COA RecWeek-style directories, AIS partner write-ups, socials) found **no active Ateneo de Manila student organization named CELA** dedicated to climate, environment, or campus sustainability.
- Near-collisions elsewhere (not AdMU green orgs): “CELA” as College of Education and Liberal Arts at other campuses; French *cela*; Certificate of Land Allocation (land tenure acronym). None map to an AdMU climate club.
- **Operational substitute stack for “campus green”:** **AIS** (institutional hub) + **SEEDS** (AVP mission integration) + **AESS** (premier environmental student org) + **BOx** (biology / biodiversity / youth climate presence) + **MEA** (corporate sustainability / systems) + **Blue Mobility** (sustainable mobility) + **OSA** sustainability program (org-wide culture). Treat “CELA” as a **label error or informal shorthand** unless the cohort has private knowledge of a micro-collective not listed publicly.

### 6. Campus waste programs (ops layer — Gargar’s physical context)

**Policy backbone**

- **2016 Sustainability Policies and Specific Guidelines** — minimize waste; restrict single-use packaging; BYO culture (straws/glasses/containers).
- **Ecological Solid Waste Management:** reduction, segregation at source, diversion via **unit-based MRFs**.
- **Laudato Si’ Plan 2022–2029** — carbon neutrality pathway; ecological SWM enhancement; student-org lifestyle integration.
- **Sustainable University Master Plan (SUMP)** — drafted 2025; BoT approval path (memo lineage into 2026); short/medium/long targets to 2050 (carbon neutrality, wastewater, biodiversity, green learning environments). First PH university sustainability master plan claim (GUIDON 2025).
- **Centralized MRF (CMRF)** listed in 2023 LSAP implementation discourse as a planned construction item; public ops still describe **unit MRFs** (LS, High School, etc.) as of AIS SWM page — treat CMRF as **planned/aspirational until a commissioning notice**.

**Ops facts (AIS Solid Waste Management program)**

| Metric / practice | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| Landfill residual (2021–2022) | **357.1 metric tons** solid waste sent to landfill from the University |
| Operators | Unit **Facilities Management and Safety Offices (FMSO)**; University Pollution Control Officer collates for DENR quarterly self-monitoring |
| Segregation UX | LS: bin opening shape codes paper vs PET/cans; HS: “bee-hive” gamified PET/can bins |
| Diversion | Recyclables, dry paper, **e-waste** sold to **accredited recyclers**; proceeds as **maintenance staff bonuses** |
| Organics | Yard + cafeteria food waste → **vermicomposting** (African nightcrawlers) at AGS + Loyola Schools; vermicast to campus gardens |
| Residuals | Accredited garbage contractor |
| Construction waste | Design/construction guidelines + contractor environment criteria |

**Waste audits (AIS × CFMO Custodial Services)**

- First Loyola Heights waste audit lineage: **June 2008** → single-use bans, BYOB, 2016 guidelines.
- **Sep–Oct 2025:** University-wide **WACS (Waste Analysis and Characterization Survey)** across AGS, AJHS, ASHS, Higher Education, dorms, Ortigas, Salcedo, Rockwell — wet-season generation snapshot; staff re-orientation on segregation; recyclables recovery via accredited third parties.
- Follow-up dry-season audit scheduled **1H 2026**.
- Finding of note: despite labeled bins, **much waste still arrives mixed** and needs re-sorting — behavior gap Gargar can partially address for **student-generated recyclables** if pilot links households/dorms/orgs to known offtake.

**Refill / circular campaigns (student-facing)**

- Refill Revolution (AIS + Back to Basics + AESS, 2022).
- ReFair / Repair–Refill–Rethink–Recover (AIS + MEA + Blue Mobility + external orgs) — clothes, e-waste, refill stalls, campus bikes.
- Food Packaging Primer + cafeteria single-use rules.

**Implication for Gargar:** Campus already has **MRF → accredited recycler** pipes and **staff bonus** incentives. Student app value is **not replacing FMSO**, but (a) **price visibility** for student-held scrap, (b) **diversion logging** for org events/dorms, (c) optional bridge to **off-campus Pasig collectors** (flagship product geography) while using campus for **user acquisition and story**. Respect accredited-recycler contracts; co-design with AIS/CFMO so pilot does not skim materials staff rely on for bonuses.

### 7. Governance & climate scaffolding (context for “why campus cares”)

- Laudato Si’ University pathway (target window often cited toward **2029** / LSAP horizon).
- Climate Action Plan + Biodiversity Action Plan processes (AIS secretariat / public consultation cadence into 2025–26).
- Race to Zero participant; RE supply shift (SolX / Shell Energy narrative 2024+); on-site solar program.
- Student body already used as LSAP co-implementers (Sanggu/COA briefings; multi-org SustainaBOx; ReFair).

Gargar’s campus story should plug into **SDG 12 (responsible consumption) + lifestyle conversion + waste diversion data**, not invent a parallel climate office.

## Campus pilot partners

Ranked for a **Gargar co-pilot** (7-day / ≤30-user pilot → showcase continuity). Roles: **host legitimacy**, **recruit users**, **host booth**, **help log kg**, **do not** operate hauling without FMSO/AIS clearance.

### Tier C — operational / do-not-bypass

| Partner | Why | Rule |
|---------|-----|------|
| **CFMO Custodial / unit FMSO / Pollution Control Officer** | Run MRFs, haulers, DENR reports, staff bonuses from recyclable sales | **Inform before** any campus collection day; never position Gargar as unauthorized scavenger of campus recyclables |
| **Accredited recyclers** (university contracts) | Legal offtake | Campus kg may stay in existing channels; Gargar’s Pasig collector directory is **off-campus product** unless a dual path is negotiated |

### Suggested co-pilot stack for Gargar (default)

1. **Recruitment engine:** BOx + AESS + MEA + MEcO (soft commits already in pilot-7-day thinking).  
2. **Institutional cover:** AIS intro + SEEDS continuity.  
3. **Culture spread:** OSA Bingo / Sustainability Week micro-challenge.  
4. **Ops hygiene:** CFMO/FMSO courtesy brief if any physical scrap moves on campus.  
5. **Product geography:** Keep **Pasig collectors + EcoWaste rates** as core product truth; use Ateneo as **user lab + narrative**, not sole offtake geography.

### One-line partner pitch (campus)

> “Gargar makes scrap rates visible and lets orgs log diversion in minutes — filling the waste-tracking gap OSA already flagged, and giving AIS/SEEDS student-generated proof for Laudato Si’ lifestyle and SUMP waste goals.”
