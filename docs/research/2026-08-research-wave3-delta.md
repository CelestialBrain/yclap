## 1. Landfill methane + disposal crisis (R01, R02, R08)

### Facts to use

- Metro Manila MSW order of magnitude: **~9,500–10,500 t/day**.
- For years disposal concentrated in **Rodriguez**, **New San Mateo**, **Navotas** private SLFs.
- **Navotas** halted active ops ~Aug 2025 (airport land pressure); **10 Apr–10 May 2026 fire** at closed NSLF attributed to **methane + heat**; ~28.6 ha burned; incomplete SCRP / gas venting cited.
- **Rodriguez (RPSL)** ~50 ha, **>3,000 t/day**; **20 Feb 2026 trash slide** (~420,000 m³); prior fires/collapses; DENR CDO / phase suspensions reported.
- **San Mateo** became diversion pressure valve after Navotas shut; ECC ~**5,000 t/day** for MM waste — host-LGU conflict classic.
- **Payatas** closed 2017; historic LFG-to-energy shows CH₄ is real and addressable — **upstream diversion still wins for students**.
- **RA 9003 @ ~25 years:** MRFs serve only ~**39% of barangays** nationally (COA-era); Metro Manila barangay MRF coverage often cited far lower (~**20%** class figures in R02); open dumps persist; penalties rarely bite.

### Gargar implication

Narrative: **scrap diversion = landfill load + methane co-benefit + safety co-benefit**, grounded in 2025–26 SLF failures. Metrics = **kg diverted**, not sellable tCO₂e.

## 3. EPR RA 11898 + plastic credits (R03, R04)

### EPR (live compliance regime)

- Targets for plastic packaging recovery of prior-year footprint: 20% (2023) → 40% (2024) → **50% (2025)** → **60% (2026)** → 70% (2027) → **80% (2028+)**.
- DENR **2026 EPR Recognition** messaging: national diversion rate **~55.98%** vs 40% target; **246M kg** claimed diverted; **1,017** entities / **201** EPR programs registered (late-2025 figures).
- **Caveat for pitch:** official diversion mixes **recycling + upcycling + co-processing**. Do not treat the headline % as pure circular recycling.

### Plastic credits / PCX — avoid as product

- SourceMaterial (2023): large share of analyzed PCX credits from **co-processing** (cement kiln fuel), not mechanical recycling.
- BFFP/GAIA “Smoke and Mirrors” integrity line still defines the debate 2024–26.
- RA 11898 language uses “plastic neutrality” for OE recovery schemes — **law language ≠ good youth product**.

**Program rule:** Study EPR as *policy context*. Ship **physical diversion + fair rates**. Never plastic-neutrality certificates.
