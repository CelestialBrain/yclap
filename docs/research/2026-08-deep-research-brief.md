- **Ancestral domain / mining / “green transition” nickel** — 2.8 Mha CADT entanglement; FPIC failures; defender risk

1. **Organic waste → landfill methane** (Metro Manila food scraps + RA 9003 gap)

4. **Heat / cooling equity** (QC: ~81% barangays high heat-risk)  
5. **Last-mile flood early-action** (not new sensors — SK playbooks + inclusive alerts)  
6. **LGU delivery tooling** (LCCAP, SWM plans, PSF access)

**Gargar as a Metro Manila / Pasig / Ateneo scrap-to-value pilot** that:

- Treats **waste diversion as climate mitigation** (methane + landfill load) **and** flood co-benefit

## 3. Philippines crisis map (2024–2026)

### 3.1 Vulnerability

- **WorldRiskIndex:** #1 globally (2024–2025)  
- **Climate Risk Index:** 7th most affected in 2024 (Germanwatch CRI 2026)  
- **OECD 2026:** GDP losses could reach ~4% by 2040 / ~17% by 2070 under high-emissions paths  
- **Historical CRI:** ~371 extremes, ~27,500 fatalities, >$35B losses (1995–2024 range cited in secondary)

### 3.2 Extremes lived recently

- **2024:** Six typhoons in ~30 days; attribution science: consecutive major typhoons more likely under warming  
- **2025 Nov:** Kalmaegi (Tino) + Fung-wong (Uwan) — high death/displacement tallies (verify final NDRRMC)  
- **Heat:** Metro Manila danger-range heat indices; school shifts online (2024–2025)  
- **2026:** Flood-then-drought narrative (habagat extremes + El Niño watch); Angat water stress headlines  

### 3.3 Policy architecture (what CCC/YCLAP sits inside)

| Instrument | Role |
|------------|------|
| **RA 9729** Climate Change Act | CCC mandate |
| **RA 10174** People’s Survival Fund | Local adaptation finance (access friction remains) |
| **NAP 2023–2050** | Adaptation backbone (8 priority sectors) |
| **NDC 2021** | 75% GHG cut vs BAU 2020–2030; **only 2.71% unconditional** |
| **RA 9003** | Ecological solid waste |
| **RA 11898** EPR | Plastic producer responsibility |
| **RA 9513** + foreign ownership reforms | RE pipeline boom |
| **CLIMA Act** | Pending accountability bill — not enacted as of late 2025 |

**SONA 2026 tilt (public analysis):** disaster infrastructure + RE; civil society critiques missing holistic climate framing and integrity of flood works.

### 3.4 Sector deep dives (problem inventory)

#### A. Adaptation & disasters
- Multi-hazard consecutive events  
- Early warning exists nationally; **last-mile action** fails  
- Flood control **corruption scandal** dominates PH X discourse 2025–26 (ghost projects, contractor concentration)  

#### B. Food, water, heat
- Rice yield sensitivity to night heat  
- El Niño agri damage (₱9.5–15B range across reporting windows)  
- Angat ~90% of Metro Manila water  

#### C. Energy & mitigation
- Coal still **~60%+** of generation  
- RE pipeline **~120 GW** claimed — pipeline ≠ plants online  
- No full coal phase-out date in planning  

#### D. Waste as climate
- Metro Manila: thousands of tons MSW/day; incomplete collection  
- Organics → **methane** (short-lived, high GWP)  
- EPR live; **implementation quality** varies  
- Plastic credits (PCX) face **co-processing / integrity** critiques  

#### E. Biodiversity & seas
- 15–23% of terrestrial vertebrates extinction risk (2025 study)  
- 4th global bleaching; WPS severe sites lost majority hard coral cover in ~2 years  
- Mangroves: historical >50–64% loss; mass planting often fails without hydrology  

#### F. Ancestral domain & extractives
- LRC SIPA 2025: **2.8 Mha** CADT under deforestation-driving tenements; **≥51% CADT area** entangled  
- Nickel mining for EVs increases local climate vulnerability (CRI *Broken Promises*, Nov 2025)  
- Carbon projects on ancestral land: opportunity **only with real FPIC + benefit-sharing** (DENR forest carbon roadmap emerging)  

#### G. Urban Metro Manila stack
| Problem | Urgency | Student agency (5 weeks) |
|---------|---------|--------------------------|
| Waste clogging waterways | Very high | **Very high** |
| Urban heat (QC high-HRI barangays) | High | **High** |
| Flood-control transparency | Very high | Medium (document/ally) |
| Informal settlement risk | Very high | Medium (invited partnership only) |
| Air / transport | High | Medium |
| Structural drainage mega-projects | Very high | Low |
| Angat water system | High | Low |

## 9. How this changes the Gargar pitch

### Old pitch risk
“Recycling marketplace app.”

### Research-backed pitch
1. **Problem:** Metro Manila waste fails RA 9003 → landfill methane + clogged waterways → worse floods; price opacity hurts households and collectors.  
2. **Science:** Waste sector is an NDC PAM; methane is high-GWP; diversion is mitigation + adaptation co-benefit.  
3. **Justice:** Informal collectors/junkshops are the real recovery system; EPR money must not bypass them.  
4. **Solution:** Gargar — transparent rates + booking path + pilot kg log.  
5. **Evidence:** EcoWaste rates, junkshop map, EPR landscape.  
6. **Not claiming:** Carbon credits, national scale, or fixing flood-control corruption alone — but **naming** basura→baha.  
7. **Ask:** Partner collectors, campus pilot, continuity via AIS/SEEDS/orgas.
