# Philippines Carbon Markets 2025–2026 — YCLAP Deep Brief

**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Question:** Should Youth CLAP students build carbon products?  
**Verdict:** **No** as a core product (study/critique OK; real-tonne trading apps not).

---

## 1. Snapshot (mid-2026)

| Track | Status | Readiness |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **DOE energy carbon credits** | DC2025-09-0018 published Oct 2025; TFECC formalized Jun 2026 | Framework live; full market plumbing incomplete |
| **DENR forest VCM** | DAO 2026-02 adopts Roadmap 2026–2030 (Jan/Feb 2026) | **Readiness**, not open trading |
| **Article 6 blueprint** | ADB-supported Blueprint; PH–SG Implementation Agreement Apr 2026 | Bilateral path opening; national registry prototyping |
| **Palawan IP deal** | CI + NCIP + Pala’wan ancestral domains (signed Nov 2024) | Pilot/development; multi-year before credit revenue |
| **Integrity / civil society** | Greenpeace: offsets ≠ substitute for real cuts; climate-fund corruption focus | High reputational risk for student “carbon” products |

**Bottom line:** Policy is *moving fast*; markets are *not open for student-scale products*. National carbon rights, benefit-sharing, and project DAO guidelines are still being built. First Article 6 pilot shortlists target ~2027.

## 2. DOE — Energy Carbon Credit Circular

### What it is
- **Instrument:** Department Circular **DC2025-09-0018** — *Providing General Guidelines for the Generation, Management, and Monitoring of Carbon Credits in the Energy Sector*
- **Signed:** ~23 Sep 2025 (Sec. Sharon S. Garin); **published:** 10 Oct 2025
- **Unit:** Carbon Credit Certificate (**CCC**) = **1 tCO₂e** reduced/removed; must be real, additional, measurable, permanent; anti–double counting rules

### Eligible activity families (must still pass a recognized methodology)
1. Voluntary early retirement of coal plants  
2. Renewable energy development  
3. Early decommissioning + RE replacement  
4. Energy efficiency  
5. Emerging zero/low-carbon tech  
6. Fuel switching / hybridization / co-firing  
7. Switch to EVs  
8. Biofuels blending  
9. Other energy mitigation as DOE-approved  

**Important:** Activities already counted under **NDC unconditional** measures are **ineligible**. A facility may not issue both a **CCC** and a **REC** for the same attributes (double issuance ban).

### Markets recognized
1. International compliance (Article 6 / **ITMOs**)  
2. Future domestic compliance / ETS  
3. Voluntary carbon market (VCM)

### Implementation body
- **Task Force on Energy Carbon Credits (TFECC)** — required under §14; institutionalized by department order ~18 Jun 2026 (EPPB-led; multi-bureau membership).

## 3. DENR — Forest Carbon DAO + Roadmap 2026–2030

### What it is
- **DAO No. 2026-02** (signed **20 Jan 2026**, Sec. Raphael P.M. Lotilla; published Manila Bulletin **10 Feb 2026**)  
- **Subject:** Adoption of the **Philippines’ Readiness in the Voluntary Forest Carbon Market: Roadmap (2026–2030)**  
- **Support:** UNDP AGCF project (Canada-funded), with DoF partners  
- **Scope:** Terrestrial forests **and** blue carbon (mangroves, seagrass)

### Four priority areas
1. Policy framework for VCM engagement (carbon rights, trading rules, project DAO still needed)  
2. Data analytics, **MRV**, forest carbon credit database + link to National Carbon Registry  
3. Institutional mechanisms + capacity (FMB terrestrial; BMB blue carbon; CCS oversight)  
4. Sustainable financing + market development (possible Trust Fund / revenue recycling with DoF)

### Honest gaps named *in the Roadmap itself*
- **Carbon rights** not yet clearly defined in PH law (state vs tenure holder vs community vs developer)  
- Overlapping tenures (public forest, ancestral domain, NIPAS)  
- No mature domestic **carbon trading** law; international sale needs Article 6 authorization + corresponding adjustment  
- Weak technical capacity; **no accredited third-party auditors** operating in-country at Roadmap writing  
- Investor accountability failures; tax treatment of carbon revenue unclear  
- Benefit-sharing guidelines still to be written  

### Timeline (roadmap)
| Year | Focus |
|------|--------|
| **2026–27** | Core enablers: EO (DENR as Article 6 focal), project guidelines DAO, registry prototype, shortlist for Article 6 pilot |
| **2028** | Mid-term review; fuller registry ops |
| **2029–30** | Scale projects; explore domestic exchange; institutionalize lessons |

### Related (still in pipeline)
- Draft **DAO** on *Guidelines Governing Establishment, Registration, and Monitoring of Voluntary Forest Carbon Credit Projects* (safeguards/registration) — not the same as the readiness DAO  
- Possible **EO** designating DENR as National Authority for Article 6 / VCM

## 4. Article 6 Blueprint + bilateral path

### National “Blueprint”
- Working name: **Blueprint for the Philippine Carbon Market Policy Framework** (sometimes “Blueprint for Carbon Trading Policy”)  
- **ADB** technical assistance; inter-agency process (consultations reported ~Apr–Dec 2025; still moving through adoption / CCC resolution track in 2026)  
- Intended as **umbrella** over energy, forest, blue carbon, VCM, and corresponding-adjusted ITMOs  
- Core design elements: integrity principles, eligible activities, **authorization**, fees/share of proceeds, **National Carbon Registry**, institutional arrangements  

### Bilateral milestone
- **30 Apr 2026:** Philippines–**Singapore** signed first **Implementation Agreement** under Article 6 (builds on Aug 2024 MOU)  
- Signals PH readiness for **high-integrity** cooperation — not instant student offtake

## 5. Palawan IP carbon deal

### Facts
- **Where:** Two ancestral domains in **Mount Mantalingahan Protected Landscape**, southern Palawan  
- **Who:** Pala’wan Indigenous leaders + **Conservation International** + **NCIP**  
- **Signed:** ~25 Nov 2024 (reported Jan 2025)  
- **Framing:** First PH carbon project **owned by Indigenous peoples** (per CI); IPs as rightful benefactors of forest carbon  
- **Threats cited:** Illegal logging/mining, palm oil pressure, land grabs; >20% forest/mangrove loss in landscape over two decades (CI)  
- **Timeline realism:** Community spent **~8 years** reaching agreement; **~25 years** forest protection commitment; carbon **revenues not immediate** (CI paying community members minimum wage for conservation work in interim)

### Why it matters for YCLAP
- Positive model: FPIC, IP ownership, long-horizon stewardship  
- Not a template for a 5-week Ateneo product  
- Shows carbon finance on ancestral land is **slow, rights-heavy, politics-heavy**

## 6. Integrity risks (PH + global)

| Risk | Why it bites in PH 2026 |
|------|-------------------------|
| **Additionality / over-crediting** | Forest baselines and REDD+ methodologies globally under fire; PH still building FREL/MRV capacity |
| **Double counting** | NDC vs ITMO vs VCM vs RECs — DOE bans CCC+REC double issue, but national registry still prototyping |
| **Carbon rights ambiguity** | Roadmap admits law does not clearly assign ownership of sequestered carbon |
| **FPIC / tenure conflict** | Ancestral domain + protected area + public forest overlaps |
| **Benefit-sharing vacuum** | Guidelines still “to develop” under roadmap |
| **Auditor gap** | Few/no in-country accredited verifiers at roadmap baseline |
| **Investor “market failure”** | Communities report signed interest that never funds |
| **Greenwashing / offset misuse** | Credits used to delay fossil exit (core Greenpeace charge) |
| **Conflicts of interest** | Global VCM: developer–standard–auditor capture (e.g. Transparency International US 2025) |
| **Delivery / permanence** | Typhoon + land-use pressure on nature-based credits |

### Global integrity signals
- ICVCM Core Carbon Principles / VCMI Claims Code — quality bar rising; low-quality credits repriced  
- Science synthesis: credits often fail as fossil substitutes (10insightsclimate 2025)  
- MSCI 2025 integrity state of market: quality premium + delivery risk

## 7. Greenpeace critiques (PH-specific)

### On DOE carbon rules (18 Oct 2025)
- Carbon markets = **false solution** if they substitute for real emission cuts  
- DOE “railroading” markets while enabling **fossil gas** and **coal moratorium exemptions**  
- Missing safeguards: **no public registry, no open data, no pass-through cost protection, no community benefit guarantee**  
- Risk: legitimizes unregulated VCM so high emitters **profit from pollution theater**  
- Demand: **just energy transition roadmap first**; any carbon finance must drive real decarbonization  

**URL:** https://www.greenpeace.org/philippines/press/68654/department-of-energy-carbon-credit-rules-not-a-license-to-profit-must-cut-emissions-now/

### Adjacent 2025–26 Greenpeace PH climate integrity line
- Flood-control / climate-tagged fund **corruption** (orders of magnitude: claims up to ~₱1T / ~US$19B potentially lost) — “climate criminals” framing  
- Reinforces: climate money without integrity fails communities  
- Example: https://www.eco-business.com/news/climate-criminals-greenpeace-philippines-warns-us19-billion-in-climate-tagged-funds-potentially-lost-to-corruption/

## 8. Should Youth CLAP build carbon products?

# **NO**

### Reasons (program-fit)

1. **Markets are readiness-stage, not product-stage.** DENR roadmap runs **2026–2030**. Project registration DAO, benefit-sharing, tax treatment, and full national registry are incomplete. A student “carbon marketplace” cannot settle real tCO₂e.  
2. **Authorization is state infrastructure.** Article 6 corresponding adjustments, DNA processes, and TFECC/DENR gatekeeping are not hackable in 5 weeks.  
3. **Integrity + justice risk is career- and brand-defining.** Pitching “offsets” invites Greenpeace-style critique and mentor pushback on additionality, double counting, and IP rights.  
4. **Wrong geography & scale.** YCLAP default is **Ateneo + one Pasig/QC node**, hero metric **kg diverted**. Utility coal retirement, industrial RE, and Mantalingahan forests are not campus pilots.  
5. **Palawan shows the real timeline:** years of FPIC + decades of stewardship before credit cash — not a Sep 12 showcase loop.  
6. **Better climate product already fits the brief:** Gargar / organic waste diversion → **landfill methane mitigation** + flood co-benefit + informal worker justice — *without* claiming sellable credits.

### What *is* allowed (if cohort insists on carbon literacy)

| OK | Not OK |
|----|--------|
| One-pager explaining DOE/DENR/Article 6 map | “Buy our carbon credits” |
| Critique of offset integrity (Science lane) | Fake MRV / estimated tonnes sold as real |
| Methane **avoided** estimate from waste diversion (transparent method, not tradable) | Tokenized forest credits without tenure/FPIC |
| Partner research on PCX / plastic credits controversy | Plastic or carbon “neutrality” claims |

### One-sentence pitch rule
**Do climate work that cuts waste and risk in Metro Manila; do not cosplay as a carbon-credit issuer.**
