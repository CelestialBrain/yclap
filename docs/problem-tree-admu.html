Climate Change Problem Tree — Ateneo de Manila University

Youth CLAP · Group Growth Task 1 · 22 August 2026

Branches — consequences if unaddressed

Environmental

Organics rot anaerobically in landfill and release methane; plastics that leak into creeks and drainage feed the basura → baha chain.

Economic

Value is destroyed — households and orgs throw away money, hauling and tipping costs are borne publicly, and collectors lose steady sorted supply.

Social / Justice

Flood and dumpsite burden falls on low-income barangays downstream, not on the campus that generated the waste. Informal workers carry the risk with the least price power.

Political

Landfill capacity keeps being consumed, RA 9003 compliance stays on paper, and LGUs are pushed toward end-of-pipe fixes instead of diversion.

Technological

Recovery infrastructure stays under-used and under-measured — no data on what is generated or diverted, so no one can size an MRF or a route.

Trunk — the problem

Recyclable and organic waste from Ateneo and its surrounding community is thrown as residual instead of diverted — even though it has market value and a nearby buyer.

Roots — why this problem is in place

R1Price opacity

Scrap has a real ₱/kg value, but whoever is holding the bottle cannot see it. With no visible value, material defaults to garbage.

R2Broken last link

Junkshops, MRFs and informal collectors already recover material. Finding one and reaching it is the friction. Capacity exists; routing does not.

R3Rule, not habit

RA 9003 mandates segregation, but enforcement is weak and bins are recombined downstream — so sorting feels pointless.

R4Framed as sanitation

Disposal is treated as somebody else's job. Nobody counts the kilograms or the carbon, so there is no feedback loop and no accountability.

Deepest root — the material's value and its climate cost are both unpriced and unmeasured at the moment of the decision.

Our solution

Make the price and the path visible at the moment of disposal — then log the kilograms.

Attacks R1 and R2 directly, and closes the missing feedback loop on R4 by making diversion measurable.
Not claiming: carbon credits or offsets · a flood-control fix (drainage is a stated co-benefit) · any collector as “verified” before we have contacted them. Every kg shown is either logged or labeled a demo seed.
