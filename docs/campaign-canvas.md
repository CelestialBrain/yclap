## 1. Problem

In Pasig / Metro Manila, recyclable material has market value — but households and students rarely see transparent rates or a clear path to a collector. Residual waste feeds landfills (methane, capacity crisis) and clogs waterways (**basura → baha**). Informal junkshops and collectors already recover materials; opacity and friction keep diversion low.

## 2. Climate framing (mentor-safe)

| Layer | Claim |
|-------|--------|
| Mitigation | Diversion cuts landfill load / methane co-benefit (waste sector) |
| Adaptation co-benefit | Less clogged drainage amplifies flood risk less (DPWH/MMDA framing) |
| Justice | Price transparency + fair path for informal recovery workers |
| **Not claiming** | Carbon credit product, national flood-control reform, plastic “neutrality” offsets, CLIMA Act dependency |

**SDGs:** 12 (primary), 13, 11 · secondary 8

## 4. Goal (SMART)

By **12 September 2026**, run a **Gargar pilot** with **≥30 users**, **≥3 field-verified collectors**, **≥100 kg** diverted (or honest proxy + plan), plus Campaign Canvas + 3-min pitch for the Innovation Showcase (Likha-Klima track).

## 5. Theory of change

If people can **see the price** and **reach a collector in one flow**, they will **sort and divert** more recyclables → less residual waste → measurable kg + ₱ story for climate + justice.

## 6. Offer

**Gargar** — reference rates (EcoWaste + research freeze), calculator, Pasig collector directory (showcase trio first), diversion log with export for pilot proof.

**Mission line:** *See the scrap price. Reach a collector. Log the diversion.*

**Flood activation:** when a flood event hits the node, drives re-activate per [`docs/pilot/flood-drive-protocol.md`](pilot/flood-drive-protocol.md) (PAGASA Orange trigger, 72-hour loop).
