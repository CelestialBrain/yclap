## 2 · Three pitfalls

Each has a one-line reason and the evidence that killed it last time.

**(a) Another recycling / transparency app.** The category is crowded and capital-hungry, and Gargar already owns the transparency wedge — a second app splits our own niche.
Evidence: `docs/research/wave3-agent/R10-circular-startup-ph.md:106` ("Do not pitch Gargar as 'the recycling app for the Philippines'"); `:100` warns off the "smart recycle" app gravity well; rejected in `docs/roadmap-rejected.md:10`.

**(b) Carbon-credit pitches, including blue carbon.** Credits need tenure, baseline, additionality, and permanence no student cohort can supply — and PH credit products sit inside an active integrity scandal.
Evidence: `docs/research/wave3-agent/R28-blue-carbon-mangrove.md:149` ("Do not build YCLAP ventures on selling mangrove credits, airline offsets, or hectare claims"); `:92` on elite capture without tenure; PCX fallout `docs/research/wave3-agent/R10-circular-startup-ph.md:57`; rejected in `docs/roadmap-rejected.md:8`.

**(c) Planting drives as flagship.** Mass planting without zone, species, tenure, and monitoring has a long documented failure record, and claiming flood/carbon impact from a bed is the mentor-flagged credibility kill — allowed at most as literacy activity, never the pilot.
Evidence: `docs/research/wave3-agent/R30-nbs-youth-scale.md:66` ("credibility kill" on wrong claim) and `:24-28` anti-scope list; failure record `docs/research/wave3-agent/R28-blue-carbon-mangrove.md:147`; house phrase + literacy-only rule `docs/roadmap-rejected.md:9`.

## 3 · What good looks like instead

Winners cluster by **mechanism, not slogan** (`docs/research/wave3-agent/R36-youth-pitch-winner-pattern.md:15,102`): a hard local problem, a loop already running at pilot scale, honest metric (30 users / 100 kg / 3 partners, `R36:94`), named beneficiary + partner, explicit non-claim when risk is high (`R36:55`), one milestone-linked ask. And innovation ≠ tech-only: low-cost creative engagement qualifies; small beats grand — ruled from the front on Aug 22 (`docs/plaud/2026-08-22-session-2-merged.md:46`), alongside slogans-don't-count and no vanity metric (`:45`).

Quick test before you propose: does it extend the rack (Gargar · EcoWaste)? Can a demoable slice ship by Sep 12 (`README.md:35`)? Does every claim survive Q&A without an unverifiable number?
