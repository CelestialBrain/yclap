# Waste that completes its journey — one page, two scales

## Purpose → outcome

**Purpose:** the same failure happens twice a day, at two scales. In Pasig, recyclables worth pesos flow to dumps because prices are invisible, collectors are unreachable, and payouts are untrusted. At Ateneo, waste is dutifully segregated at events, then recombined downstream because nobody owns what happens after the bin. Sorting was never the bottleneck. Completing the journey is.

**Outcome:** a Pasig household sees today's scrap price, reaches a verified collector in one flow, and logs the diversion. An Ateneo organizer estimates an event's waste before it happens, deploys colored-bag stations, and sees exactly which partner takes each stream after. One engine, both scales, still running after YCLAP ends.

## The idea

**City scale (Gargar, already built):**
1. Live rate board: see the price per material
2. Collector directory: reach a verified buyer on the map
3. Diversion ledger: weigh-ins, transparent payouts, badges, LGU-grade data

**Campus scale (three new modules on the same engine):**
1. Waste calculator: attendance plus sponsor mix in; kilos, bags, bins, haul trips out
2. Colored-bag kit: deployable hangable stations where people actually eat and gather
3. Partner map: every stream routed to a named downstream taker

**Shared proof:** Gargar's ledger turns both scales into numbers, kilos diverted, pesos returned, percentage surviving to recycling. That baseline is the study nobody has done.

## What changes

| | Today | After |
|---|---|---|
| Pasig household | guesses, hoards, or trashes recyclables | sees price, books a pickup, gets paid fairly |
| Collector | invisible, haggled, unrecorded | badged, findable, banked |
| Event organizer | three cans, cleanup after | estimate first, kits deployed, actuals logged |
| Segregated waste | recombined into landfill | routed to named partners, tracked |
| LGU plans | compliance on paper, zero data | real diversion numbers feeding RA 9003 and LCCAP reporting |
| SOM forest | dying understory, mystery litter | evidence exhibit: our sampling showed the trash link |
